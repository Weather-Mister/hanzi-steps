# Lesson 1 — My Family

## New
- 家
- 家人
- 爸爸
- 媽媽

## Goal
Understand and identify immediate family.

## Core Sentences

**這是我的家人。**  
*Zhè shì wǒ de jiārén.*  
This is my family.

**這是我爸爸。**  
*Zhè shì wǒ bàba.*  
This is my dad.

**這是我媽媽。**  
*Zhè shì wǒ māma.*  
This is my mom.

## Requirements
- Reuse already-taught **這、是、我、的**.
- Do not introduce additional grammar.
- Do not test **哥哥／姐姐／妹妹** yet.

## Suggested Activity Order
1. Character introduction
2. Trace / handwriting
3. Components
4. Word matching
5. Meaning recall
6. Sentence recognition
7. Short sentence building

# Lesson 2 — Brothers and Sisters

## New
- 哥哥
- 姐姐
- 妹妹

## Goal
Talk about siblings using already-known possession and classifiers.

## Reuse
- 有／沒有
- 個
- 兩

## Core Sentences

**我有一個哥哥。**  
*Wǒ yǒu yí ge gēge.*  
I have one older brother.

**我有兩個妹妹。**  
*Wǒ yǒu liǎng ge mèimei.*  
I have two younger sisters.

**我沒有姐姐。**  
*Wǒ méiyǒu jiějie.*  
I don't have an older sister.

## Correctness Requirement
For **two + classifier**, use **兩個**, not **二個**.

# Lesson 4 — Family Photos

## New
- 照片
- 張

## Goal
Introduce the photo classifier **張**.

## Explain Before Testing

Teach explicitly:

> **張 zhāng** is a measure word used for flat objects such as photographs, paper, and tables. For photos, use **number + 張 + 照片**.

## Core Phrases

**一張照片**  
*yì zhāng zhàopiàn*  
one photo

**兩張照片**  
*liǎng zhāng zhàopiàn*  
two photos

**這張照片**  
*zhè zhāng zhàopiàn*  
this photo

## Core Sentence

**我有兩張照片。**  
*Wǒ yǒu liǎng zhāng zhàopiàn.*  
I have two photos.

## Restrictions
- Do not introduce **幾** yet.
- Save fuller quantity questions for Unit 7.

# Lesson 5 — Whose Photo?

## New Vocabulary
None.

## Goal
Combine previously learned **的** with **誰** and **照片**.

## Core Sentences

**這是誰的照片？**  
*Zhè shì shéi de zhàopiàn?*  
Whose photo is this?

**這是我姐姐的照片。**  
*Zhè shì wǒ jiějie de zhàopiàn.*  
This is my older sister's photo.

**這是我家人的照片。**  
*Zhè shì wǒ jiārén de zhàopiàn.*  
This is a photo of my family.

## Restrictions
- Treat this primarily as reinforcement of **的**.
- Do not introduce omission of the noun after **的**.
- Do not jump ahead to later textbook grammar.

# Lesson 6 — 我媽媽, Not Always 我的媽媽

## New Vocabulary
None.

## Goal
Teach the Lesson 2 nuance that **的** is often omitted with close family relationships and home.

## Explain Before Testing

Teach:

> With close family relationships, Chinese often drops **的** after a pronoun.

Examples:

**我爸爸**  
my dad

**我媽媽**  
my mom

**我哥哥**  
my older brother

**我家**  
my home / my family

Also explain:

> **我的爸爸** is grammatical, but **我爸爸** is very natural in ordinary conversation.

Contrast with:

**我的照片**  
my photo

This should make clear that **的** is not simply omitted everywhere.

## Correctness Requirement
Do not teach **我的爸爸** as wrong.

None.

Mix all Unit 6 material with earlier units.

**這是誰？**  
→ **這是我媽媽。**

**你有哥哥嗎？**  
→ **有，我有一個哥哥。**

**兩＿＿照片**  
→ **張**

Translate:

**This is my older sister's photo.**  
→ **這是我姐姐的照片。**

Natural expression check:

**我媽媽** ✓

Do not mark **我的媽媽** as grammatically incorrect.

## Final Comprehension Sequence

**這是我的家人。這是我爸爸。這是我媽媽。**

The learner should be able to understand this using only material from Units 1–6.
