# Book 1 Unit 9 — Sports, Music & Choices

Primary authority: the supplied *A Course in Contemporary Chinese 1*, Lesson 3,
printed pp. 46, 48, 52–55 and 59 (PDF pp. 73, 75, 79–82 and 86).
Vocabulary and pinyin on pp. 46/48 and the grammar on pp. 52/54/55 were checked
against the rendered pages. Explanations and exercises are original combinations
of source-supported language. This continues the deferred activity/choice material
after the Units 7–8 rebalance; it does not repeat their invitation sequence.

Six new words/expressions, eleven new characters, six teaching lessons plus review,
122 steps total. Every new character has introduction, trace, completion and
independent recall; selected characters also receive assembly or component quizzes.
All twelve existing activity types are used. Fourteen steps explicitly retrieve
material from earlier units, with familiar vocabulary/grammar also reused throughout
the new applications. No teaching lesson exceeds three new characters or twenty steps.

New grammar extensions: 不常 versus 不喜歡; 也常/也不常 ordering; alternatives with
還是; activity-first topic/comment sentences; two activity topics followed by 都喜歡.
Music/exercise/sport cards teach vocabulary in usable patterns before assessment.
還是 choice practice follows the textbook dialogue and survey on printed pp. 47/59.
The review introduces no vocabulary or grammar. 樂 is read yuè in 音樂; 還 is hái
in 還是. Pinyin uses tone marks and preserves dictionary third tones.

Six characters (打 籃 球 踢 足 還) had standalone practice retained from the earlier
rebalance but were not formally taught. Their character records and geometry move
from `course/legacy` into this module without changes to the records. Existing
practice/checkpoint hashes remain identical. Five new Taiwan glyphs come from
AnimCJK `graphicsZhHant.txt`, with selected original source entries and attribution
in `public/stroke-source/unit-nine-*` and `public/credits.txt`.

Still deferred: baseball (打棒球); parts of day and meals (早上, 晚上, 吃, 晚飯,
菜, 越南); proposal + 好不好; broader adverb combinations and 都不 versus 不都;
the earlier deferred Lesson 2 descriptions and modifier + 的 + noun. This unit
does not claim to complete all of textbook Lesson 3. Baseball is held for a later
unit rather than introducing an unverified handwriting glyph.

Validation: curriculum generation/check; targeted Unit 9 navigation, checkpoints,
character practice and original-content regression gate; TypeScript; production
build. No UI, authentication, progress, streak, rendering or deployment code changes.
