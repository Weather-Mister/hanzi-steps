explanation of the approved close-family pattern precedes the first such exercise;
Lesson 6 retains its dedicated explanation and contrast with 我的照片. This avoids
testing the pattern before teaching it without introducing extra grammar.

Photo context: 的 can connect a photo with its owner or its subject. The supplied
family-photo example explicitly describes who is pictured; the explanation does
not claim that 的 always identifies either the owner or the subject.
