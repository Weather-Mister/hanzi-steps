# Book 2 Unit 2 — Nearby places and distance

Primary source: user-supplied `book 2.pdf`, A Course in Contemporary Chinese 2, Lesson 1. This is the next app-sized slice of Lesson 1, not textbook Lesson 2 and not completion of Lesson 1.

## Scope and source mapping

- Printed p. 3 / PDF p. 30: bank, convenience store, and the nearby existence question in the Traditional dialogue. 銀行 and 附近 are foundation words used by this dialogue, explicitly taught here because the app has not taught them yet.
- Printed p. 5 / PDF p. 32: 超商, pronunciation chāo-shāng. The app follows its existing convention of joining syllables within vocabulary words: chāoshāng.
- Printed pp. 13–14 / PDF pp. 40–41: 離 distance pattern; 近 and 遠, adjective negation after the reference point, A-not-A and 嗎 questions.
- All four PDF pages were visually inspected to resolve OCR errors and separate the Traditional text from the parallel Simplified text.
- Place + 有 / 沒有, 在 location, and 有沒有 receive explicit foundation explanations before testing. Existing words and forms are reused without counting them as new.
- All short practice sentences are original combinations of the sourced patterns and already taught vocabulary. They do not assert real geographic distances around NTNU.

## Teaching sequence

1. Bank: 銀行; reuse the directions question and 在 location.
2. Convenience store: 超商; explicitly teach place + 有 / 沒有.
3. Around here: 附近 and 這附近有沒有…？
4. Near/far: 近 and 遠 with positive adjective statements.
5. Reference point: A 離 B 很近 / 很遠.
6. Questions/negation: 遠不遠 / 遠嗎, 不遠 / 不近; contrast with absence.
7. Review: only previously explained material.

Six new vocabulary entries, eight new handwriting characters, 108 activities. All twelve existing activity types are retained. No new grammar or characters are introduced in review.

Pronunciation: 銀行 yínháng (not xíng); 附近 fùjìn; 近 jìn; 遠 yuǎn; 離 lí. Dictionary third tones remain written as third tones. 不 is bù before yuǎn, bú before jìn, and light bu inside 遠不遠. Device audio for 行 is given the context 銀行 to avoid the isolated character's alternative reading; standalone 行 is not used in listening tests.
