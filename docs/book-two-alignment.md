Scope: the opening directions slice, not the complete textbook lesson. Six lessons and one review; 14 new handwriting characters and 16 vocabulary entries (including foundation words and multi-character expressions).

Source mapping (printed pages; PDF page = printed page + 27):

- Source pages 2, 5, 8 and 9 were visually inspected to resolve OCR errors. Traditional Taiwan text is used, not the parallel Simplified Chinese text.

Pronunciation: 師大 Shīdà; 從 cóng; 往 wǎng; 轉 zhuǎn when turning; 一直 yìzhí with 一 tone change. Other third tones use dictionary spellings, following the existing course convention. 師大 is NTNU, not NTU.
