# Book 1 Unit 8 — Let’s Make a Plan Together

> Historical record. See [current rebalanced scope](book-one-unit-seven-eight-rebalance.md).

Primary authority: the supplied A Course in Contemporary Chinese Book 1 PDF. Printed pages 44–57 (PDF pages 71–84), focusing on the remaining Lesson 3 dialogue vocabulary and conversation functions. Vocabulary pages 46, 48 and 49 were visually checked against the scan, including tone marks. All learner explanations and practice are original.

## Scope and progression

The live Units 1–7 were inventoried first. Unit 7 already teaches weekend activities, sports, 常, 還是 and 吧. Units 4–5 already teach 想, 要, 學, 中文, 聽, 會 and positive–不–positive questions. None is counted as a new word here. Unit 8 moves from naming activities to timing, evaluating, inviting, negotiating and confirming a shared plan.

| Lesson | New contribution | Textbook basis (printed pages) |
|---|---|---|
| When Are We Going? | 今天, 明天, 早上, 晚上; day before part of day | 46, 48–51 |
| What Do You Think? | 覺得 + statement; 好玩 / 不好玩; opinion versus desire | 44–46, 52–53; basic adjective negation applied to 好玩 |
| Ask Someone to Join | 一起 versus 都; proposal + 怎麼樣; accepting with 好啊 | 44–48, 51, 57 |
| Have Dinner Together | 吃, 晚飯; extending 要不要 to a shared activity; 好不好 checks agreement | 47–49, 56–57 |
| What Can This Help You Do? | 可以 for possibility; activity as the sentence topic; contrast with previously taught 會 | 47–48 |
| Agree on a Plan | Decline, counteroffer, distinguish an opinion from acceptance, confirm time and activity | 56–57; recombinations of taught dialogue patterns |
| Review | Independent retrieval and conversation comprehension; no new teaching | Previously introduced content only |

12 new vocabulary entries: 今天 jīntiān, 明天 míngtiān, 早上 zǎoshàng, 晚上 wǎnshàng, 覺得 juéde, 好玩 hǎowán, 一起 yìqǐ, 怎麼樣 zěnmeyàng, 好啊 hǎo a, 吃 chī, 晚飯 wǎnfàn, 可以 kěyǐ. 好啊 is counted as one new expression; 好 itself is familiar. 好不好 is an extension of the known positive–negative question form, not a new vocabulary entry. 一起 follows the textbook’s surface-tone spelling yìqǐ. 早上 and 晚上 retain the fourth-tone shàng shown in the source.

146 steps: 28 / 20 / 20 / 25 / 17 / 15 / 21. Of 115 assessed practice steps, 30 (26.1%) retrieve material from earlier units and 85 (73.9%) practice new or meaningfully extended content. Introductions, grammar explanations and phrase presentations are excluded from the ratio. Revision steps are explicitly recorded by stable ID, spaced through all lessons; no lesson is wholly repetition. Repeated practice of new Unit 8 material is practice within this unit, not counted as retrieval from earlier units.

8 selected writing targets: 今 明 早 玩 起 晚 飯 可. All receive individual shape/component explanations, tracing and independent recall. Other vocabulary spellings are explicitly taught as complete words before reading or sentence exercises, without assuming handwriting mastery. 吃 is a reading word here; the source Taiwan handwriting dataset has no 吃 record, so this unit uses the verified 飯 as the meal-related writing target. Existing Book 2 character records such as 怎 remain untouched and are not assumed known to Book 1 learners.

Geometry: unmodified records from https://github.com/parsimonhi/animCJK, graphicsZhHant.txt and dictionaryZhHant.txt, retrieved 2026-09-10. Selected originals are retained under public/stroke-source/unit-eight-*.txt under the repository’s existing licenses. Source geometry was rendered and inspected. 起 uses the Taiwan form with 走 + 巳; descriptive labels follow that form. Visible groups are shape cues rather than invented etymologies.

Deferred: additional sport/music vocabulary, cuisine/nationality vocabulary, object topic-fronting, combined adverbs and 都不/不都 scope. They are not silently introduced in examples or tests. This is a targeted continuation, not a claim that all of Lesson 3 is exhausted.
