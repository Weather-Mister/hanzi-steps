## Bounded progression

Six lessons plus review: next intersection → first/second → traffic lights → going past → then → complete route. The last lesson combines the new patterns and confirms an instruction with already-taught 嗎. It introduces no further grammar. Eight new vocabulary entries include the reusable 第 and 二 and the explicitly taught forms 第一/第二; these are not eight unrelated semantic topics.

New characters, in teaching order: 下 二 第 紅 綠 燈 過 再. The earlier cumulative index contains none of these. Every new vocabulary character is covered. 紅/綠/燈 have their component meanings explained to support the full word 紅綠燈; independent color-description grammar is not introduced.

Important distinctions: 第二 is ordinal, 兩個 is quantity; 第一 keeps yī; 下一個 uses spoken yí before gè; 綠 is lǜ, not lù; 過 is the main action before a landmark, not an experiential suffix; 再 sequences planned actions while 在 marks location. All example routes are fictional and do not claim real distances or street geography.

Each new character receives an individual shape/component explanation and introduction, tracing, building, completion, and independent handwriting. All twelve established activity types are used. The review introduces no new characters, vocabulary, phrases, or rules. 再 is not tested through isolated listening because it is homophonous with 在.
