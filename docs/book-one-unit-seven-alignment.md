Corrections to the proposed prompt:

- 也, 都, 看, 去 are already taught in Book 1. Their broader uses are extensions.
- 弟弟 is not present in live Units 1–6. Do not assume it was taught; use the approved collective 兄弟 and existing family words instead.
- Match the book’s piàoliàng and xǐhuān; fángzi has a light final syllable; xiōngdì / jiěmèi retain the shown tones. 個 is displayed as light ge in counting examples, matching the textbook, without changing the old standalone gè record.
- Teach time position and 去 + activity before testing weekend plans.
- Label 常 as often, not an exact translation of usually.
- Explain no 嗎 in basic 幾/什麼 and 還是 questions. A yes/no question still uses 嗎.
- Constrain time position explicitly where the existing exact-answer sentence builder needs one of two grammatical orders.
- Keep topic-fronting, combined adverbs, negation-scope distinctions, 可以, 一起, 覺得, 怎麼樣, and other deeper Lesson 3 content for later.
