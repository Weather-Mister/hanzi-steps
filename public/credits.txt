Hanzi Steps — Book 1, Units 1–5

Lessons and English explanations are original to this project. Common words and
phrases include 你好, 學生, 我是學生, and 你是學生嗎.

The interface contains no AI-generated images. Character drawings are rendered
directly from licensed stroke geometry.

Component notes identify modern visible parts, meaning clues, and sound clues.
Memory aids are labelled as such and are not presented as literal historical
etymologies. The notes for 我 and 是 deliberately avoid a made-up meaning equation.

Audio is device speech synthesis, with a Taiwanese Mandarin voice when available.
No audio service key is required. If no suitable voice is available, a reading
alternative is offered and marked as supported practice.

Vocabulary selection in Units 2–4 is guided by the Sinica Corpus word-frequency table
in Word List with Accumulated Word Frequency in Sinica Corpus, Table 2.
https://www.aclclp.org.tw/doc/wlawf_abstract.pdf
The sample gives the 100 most frequent word/POS entries in Sinica 3.0 (5 million
words). Some spellings recur with different parts of speech. We count each taught
surface word once, not each character or repeated POS entry. This is a guide to
common vocabulary, not a universal ranking of every form of spoken Mandarin.
Everyday extras (你好, 嗎, 你們, 書, 本, 小, 這裡, 那裡, 哪裡, 看, 中文, 英文, 學, 聽) are taught alongside
the frequency-list words.

Unit 4 adds 13 words/forms, 13 written characters, six lessons, and a review.
It contributes 7 further unique surface words from the frequency table:
去, 來, 和, 說, 想, 要, 會. The other six are supporting everyday words.
學 was previously taught as a character inside 學生; Unit 4 teaches it as an
independent verb. 中文, 英文, and 哪裡 each count once, not once per character.
Pronunciations of 和 as and (hé and Taiwan hàn): Taiwan Ministry of Education
Revised Mandarin Chinese Dictionary.
https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=4733&la=0&powerMode=0
說 (shuō), Traditional spelling and Taiwan stroke count: Taiwan Ministry of
Education Concise Mandarin Dictionary.
https://dict.concised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=35509&la=0&powerMode=0
