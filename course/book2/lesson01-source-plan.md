# Book 2, source Lesson 1 — curriculum plan

Source: *A Course in Contemporary Chinese*, Book 2 textbook, Lesson 1, printed pp. 1–22 (PDF pp. 28–49). Checked the Traditional dialogue (pp. 2–3), vocabulary I (pp. 4–5), reading and vocabulary II (pp. 6–7), grammar I–V (pp. 8–14), tasks and self-check (pp. 19–22). The five source grammar points are 從…往…, evaluative V 起來, ongoing V 著, simultaneous 一邊…一邊…, and distance with 離.

| Hanzi Steps | Focus | Source coverage | First-owned words/forms | New characters |

Book 1 already owns 往, 附近, 應該, 本, 過, 一直 and 便利商店; these stay review. 過 expands here from celebrating a birthday to crossing a road. Source proper names are used in their actual direction context. Lesson 1 includes 走 as “go/walk (toward a destination)” and not “arrive.” The phrase 聽起來 is an evaluative impression, distinct from the Book 1 directional/resultative uses of 起來. 著 indicates a continuing state/action, whereas 一邊…一邊… relates two simultaneous actions. For distance, negative and questions target 遠/近, never *不離. Unit 3's eight formal words are a deliberate boundary: it teaches the reading's setup and the ongoing 著 pattern before the simultaneous/distance material in Unit 4.

Each unit has six teaching lessons and a review. A word is explained on a teaching card before its first assessment; character tracing/building follows the first word card. Characters and word ownership are assigned once. The unit reviews reuse only Book 1 and earlier Book 2 material. Actual textbook images and paragraphs are not copied into the app; examples and activities are original source-aligned adaptations.


## Learner self-containment repair (2026-09-27)

The original implementation did not meet the self-containment claim above: one
cumulative question required an absent dialogue, incidental notes pointed to an
unseen reading, and repeated model cards did not explain their language.
`SELF_CONTAINED_CONTENT.md` records the corrected source boundary and authoring
contract. Every question now supplies its needed input; short practice exchanges
and passages are shown with pinyin, translations and explanations. The vocabulary
and character ownership counts are unchanged. Existing activity positions are
preserved, with appended practice and historical completion bounds.
