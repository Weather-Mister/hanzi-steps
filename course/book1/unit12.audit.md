# Book 1 Unit 12 — How Much Altogether?

Verified 2026-09-12 against the supplied *A Course in Contemporary Chinese 1*.
Printed pages 66–67 supply the price/total expressions, 70–71 the money measure,
and 80 the number-building progression. The relevant vocabulary and number
page images were inspected for Traditional forms and tone marks.

## Scope and pacing

- Six teaching lessons and one review, 139 activities.
- 13 NEW vocabulary words/forms: 二 三 四 五 六 七 八 九 十 錢 塊 多少 一共.
- 14 first-time character cards: 二 三 四 五 六 七 八 九 十 錢 塊 多 少 共.
  Most are simple numerals; the workload is spread across the six lessons.
- Progression: 2–4 and 二 versus 兩; 5–7; 8–10 and numbers 11–99; prices with
  塊（錢）; questions with 多少錢; totals and a purchase conversation.
- 一, cup counting, drink ordering, sizes, takeout/dine-in, and acknowledgments
  are review. Composed numbers such as 三十五 are not counted as new words.
- Number pinyin includes shí’èr / èrshíyī; 多少 follows the textbook’s
  duōshǎo and 一共 its spoken yígòng. Character 共 has dictionary gòng.
- All prices are fictional practice prices in NT dollars. All taught and
  assessed amounts stay below 100. 百, 零, larger numbers, change/payment
  expressions and the remaining Lesson 4 shopping grammar are deferred.
- The user’s flexible preference for approximately 10–13 new vocabulary items
  per unit is recorded in ADDING_A_UNIT.md, without imposing a fixed quota.

## Moving 二 earlier

二 was formerly first declared in Book 2 Unit 3. Its vocabulary and character
ownership now belongs to Book 1 Unit 12; the Book 2 declaration is REVIEW.
The exact handwriting geometry, existing meaning, parts, and practice ID are
retained. The shared card note now explains the number without assuming 第.

Book 2 Unit 3’s lesson data, activity order/IDs, grammar rules, phrases, guided
characters and unit metadata are unchanged. Its 二 practice is still present.
Its original source/attribution entry remains in the Book 2 source records.
