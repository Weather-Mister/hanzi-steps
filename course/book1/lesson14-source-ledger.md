## 7. Grammar ledger

### B1L14-G001 — Time-Duration after verbal 了

Source: printed pp. 306–307 / PDF pp. 333–334.

Function: duration of a **completed** activity.

Core examples:
- 媽媽的朋友在臺北玩了三天。
- 老師在美國住了一年。
- 李小姐在語言中心工作了一個月。

Structure:
- verbal 了 followed by a time duration;
- transitive Verb + Object + repeated Verb + 了 + Duration:
  - 他租房子租了半年。
  - 他住臺北住了三年。
- when object/location is fronted, source examples do not repeat the verb:
  - 房子他只租了半年。
  - 臺北他只住了三年。

Question/drill coverage:
- 嗎
- 是不是
- 多久
- 幾個月 / 幾個鐘頭
- repeated-verb questions such as 打網球打了幾個鐘頭;
- confirmation prompts with **吧** and **對不對**, e.g. 你在紐約住了兩年吧？ / 你昨天看電視看了三個鐘頭，對不對？;
- the response model **我一共學了五個月。**, which makes 一共 source support that must be dependency-checked.

**Required distinction:** this is completed duration.

### B1L14-G002 — Completion-to-date with Double 了

Source: printed pp. 307–308 / PDF pp. 334–335.

Function: duration accumulated up to the time of speaking. The action may or may not continue depending on context.

Core examples:
- 她已經在臺灣玩了一年了。
- 陳小姐在美國住了五年了。
- 我工作了兩個月了。
- 這間房子，他已經租了半年了。
- 他們學中文學了三個星期了。

Question/drill coverage includes 多久了 and contextual 已經.

**Required distinction:**
- G001 = completed duration.
- G002 = duration accumulated through the speech point; continuation is context-dependent.

### B1L14-G003 — 快／要／快要 … 了

Source: printed pp. 308–309 / PDF pp. 335–336.

Function: imminent event/change.

Core examples:
- 快下雨了。
- 電影要結束了。
- 爸爸快要到家了。

Question examples:
- 你媽媽的生日快到了嗎？
- 比賽要開始了嗎？
- 哥哥的女朋友快要回法國了嗎？

Usage:
- Taiwan source preference: disyllabic **快要**.
- Restriction: explicit time word + **快要** is blocked by the source rule:
  - *他明天快要回來了.

The drill requires inference from contextual evidence such as dates, current time, arrival estimates, and strong wind.

### B1L14-G004 — Comparison with 更

Source: printed pp. 309–310 / PDF pp. 336–337.

Function: a degree greater than an established or understood baseline — “even more so.”

The source first illustrates standalone 更 with **星期天我更忙。**, meaning the speaker is even busier on Sunday than normally, then states that Lesson 14 introduces 更 inside 比 comparisons.

Core examples:
- 他很高，他哥哥比他更高。
- 今年比去年更冷。
- 我覺得芒果比西瓜更好吃。

Structure:
- 更 is an adverb before a state verb;
- Lesson 14 teaches it inside 比 comparisons;
- source says 是不是 is typical in questions.

Question examples:
- 這次的颱風是不是比上次的更大？
- 在學校上網是不是比在家裡更快？

### B1L14-G005 — Inferior comparison with 沒有

Source: printed pp. 310–314 / PDF pp. 337–341.

Core form: **A 沒有 B 那麼／這麼 + State Verb**.

Function: A is not as [property] as B.

Examples:
- 哥哥沒有爸爸那麼高。
- 火車沒有高鐵那麼快。
- 我的中文沒有老師的那麼好。
- 臭豆腐沒有牛肉麵這麼好吃。
- 甜點沒有小籠包那麼好做。
- 這次的颱風沒有上次的那麼可怕。

Source requirements:
- normally negative with 沒（有）;
- 那麼 / 這麼 may sometimes be omitted;
- positive **A 有 B 那麼／這麼 + State Verb** is rare except in **嗎 questions**;
- questions use **有沒有**;
- 今天有沒有昨天熱？ carries the source-described assumption that yesterday was fairly hot.

Three-way source contrast:
- equal: **A 跟 B 一樣 + State Verb**
- inferior: **A 沒有 B 那麼 + State Verb**
- superior: **A 比 B + State Verb**

Source cross-reference: the textbook identifies the equal-degree **跟…一樣…** pattern as introduced in Lesson 13 and the superior-degree **比…** pattern as introduced in Lesson 8.

## 8. Pragmatics / source usage

- **B1L14-P001** — 更 contributes an “even more” baseline, not merely neutral “more.”
- **B1L14-P002** — 有沒有 B 那麼…? comparison questions can presuppose that B has a salient degree of the property.
- **B1L14-P003** — In 開始下雪了吧？, the source English support renders the proposition as “It has probably started snowing (there).” The source therefore supports an inferential/probability reading for this occurrence of 吧; do not generalize a broader particle rule from this lesson alone.
- **B1L14-P004** — 呢 occurs in 妳怎麼沒帶傘呢？. The source English support renders the contextual why-question naturally but gives no separate gloss or explicit grammar rule for 呢, so this occurrence must remain dialogue-context support rather than a newly source-defined pragmatics rule.
- **B1L14-P005** — 慢走 / 小心慢走 is a conventional leave-taking/care formula.
- **B1L14-P006** — 快要 is explicitly identified by the textbook as the Taiwan-preferred disyllabic form in the imminence construction.

## 12. Culture

### B1L14-CUL001 — Typhoon Days-Off

Source: printed p. 320 / PDF p. 347.

The source reading presents textbook-era Taiwanese typhoon-day / school-and-work-suspension practice.

Source details:
- average wind speed class 7 or higher **or** gusts class 10 or higher;
- expected to pass through an area within the next four hours;
- school and work cancelled until the storm passes;
- the source says the decision power was given to the Directorate-General of Personnel Administration in 1993;
- the DGPA used Central Weather Bureau forecasts and local-government suggestions;
- the reading then explains that the DGPA no longer decided for the entire nation and that decisions had been vested in regional governments;
- learners were directed to local news and then-current official/contact information for updates.

**Freshness constraint:** this page is authoritative as source coverage, not as a current-2026 administrative reference. Present-day learner-facing claims require separate verification.
