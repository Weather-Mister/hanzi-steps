# Unit 34 source coverage plan

Authoritative source: *A Course in Contemporary Chinese 1*, Lesson 12 「你在臺灣學多久的中文？」 / “How Long Will You Be Studying Chinese in Taiwan?”, Dialogue I and Vocabulary I (printed pp. 246–249) plus Grammar I 先…再… (printed p. 252).

## Scope decision

Unit 34 is the first Hanzi Steps slice of Lesson 12. It covers the opening of Dialogue I through the scholarship/grades exchange and the first grammar point, 先…再…. It does **not** claim to complete Lesson 12.

The learner arc is:

- plan how long to study;
- repair the previously untaught place name 臺灣 before assessing it;
- distinguish 久 “long (time)” from 時間 “time”;
- use 先…再… to sequence two events;
- talk about study at a 語言中心 and 大學 with 念 and 需要;
- understand 花 as “spend (time or money)” in the cost reaction;
- talk about 獎學金 and 成績 while retrieving the earlier 要是…就… conditional.

The unit has 12 formal NEW vocabulary items and 13 first-taught characters, six teaching lessons, and a 22-step review.

## Grammar I: 先…再…

The textbook defines 先…再… as the temporal sequence of two consecutive events. It explicitly illustrates past and future sequences, and its drills also include habitual sequencing such as 每天….

Unit 34 therefore teaches:

- 先 before Event 1;
- 再 before Event 2;
- the second subject may remain understood when it is the same as the first;
- 再 in this construction means “then / next,” not merely “again.”

The final review independently assesses the pattern and contrasts it with the already-taught 要是…就… conditional.

## Handwriting source

All 13 new characters use exact Unicode codepoints declared through the repository AnimCJK hydrator. Traditional Hant rows are preferred. If absent, project policy permits fallback only to the identical Unicode character; no Simplified-character substitution is allowed.

Current fallbacks:
- 臺: identical codepoint from the Hans dataset;
- 灣: identical codepoint from the Ja dataset;
- 獎: identical codepoint from the Ja dataset.

For 獎, the structural decomposition is **將 + 犬**. The final dot belongs to the four-stroke 犬 bottom component.
