# Unit 34 source coverage plan

Authoritative source: *A Course in Contemporary Chinese 1*, Lesson 12 「你在臺灣學多久的中文？」 / “How Long Will You Be Studying Chinese in Taiwan?”, especially Dialogue I and Vocabulary I (printed pp. 246–249) plus Grammar I 先…再… (printed p. 252).

## Scope decision

Unit 34 is the first Hanzi Steps slice of Lesson 12. It keeps a coherent learner arc rather than attempting to force all of Lesson 12 into one oversized unit:

- planning how long to study;
- the place name 臺灣 as a prerequisite repair;
- 久 / 時間 for duration;
- 先…再… for sequence;
- 念, 大學, 語言中心, 需要 for the study plan;
- 花, 獎學金, 成績 for the cost/scholarship exchange.

The unit has 12 formal NEW vocabulary items and 13 first-taught characters. Six teaching lessons plus a 22-step review are retained.

## Grammar

### 先…再…
The textbook says this pattern presents two consecutive events and can describe either past or future sequences. Unit 34 teaches:
- 先 immediately before Event 1;
- 再 immediately before Event 2;
- shared subjects are normally not repeated before 再;
- 再 here means “then / next,” not only “again.”

The review independently tests the rule and contrasts it with the earlier 要是…就… conditional.

## Handwriting source

All 13 new characters use exact Unicode codepoints declared through the repository AnimCJK hydrator. Traditional Hant rows are preferred. If absent, the existing project policy permits same-codepoint fallback only; no Simplified-character substitution is allowed. 臺 uses the identical 臺 codepoint from the Hans dataset, while 灣 and 獎 use the identical codepoints from the Ja dataset because Hant rows are absent.
