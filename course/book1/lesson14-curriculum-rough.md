# Book 1 Lesson 14 — Curriculum Architecture Pass 1: Rough Split

Inputs:
- validated `lesson14-source-ledger.md`;
- dependency gate PASS in `lesson14-dependency-ledger.md` / `lesson14-dependency-audit.md`;
- Hanzi Steps pacing rule: normally around 10–13 genuinely new words/forms per unit, six teaching lessons plus one review, and no prerequisite tested before teaching.

This is the first architecture pass only. It does not freeze activity topology or implementation.

## Candidate unit counts considered

### Two units — rejected

Lesson 14 has:
- 30 formal NEW lexical entries;
- 3 lexical EXPANSION entries;
- 21 non-deferred NEW formal characters;
- 5 source grammar targets;
- 4 lesson outcomes;
- 5 source classroom-transfer tasks;
- typhoon culture.

A two-unit split would force roughly 15 new lexical items per unit before counting expansions, with 10+ new characters and multiple grammar systems in each. That exceeds the established pacing target and would make reviews too broad.

### Four units — rejected

A four-unit split would average fewer than eight formal NEW lexical items per unit and would either:
- create a thin weather/seasons or final comparison unit;
- split Dialogue II unnaturally;
- or manufacture boundaries around individual grammar rules.

The source and dependency graph do not require that fragmentation.

### Three units — selected

Three units preserve coherent communicative arcs while keeping lexical load near the established range:

| Unit | Rough arc | Formal NEW | Lexical EXPANSION | NEW formal chars | Formal grammar |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 42 | weather, snow/rain, four seasons, preferences | 11 | 0 | 8 | none; one support bridge |
| 43 | experiences, completed duration, duration-to-now, plans and imminence | 7 | 3 | 6 | G001–G003 |
| 44 | typhoons, caution, intensified/inferior comparison, culture and capstone | 12 | 0 | 7 | G004–G005 |

This accounts for all 30 formal NEW lexical entries, all 3 lexical expansions, and all 21 non-deferred NEW formal characters.
