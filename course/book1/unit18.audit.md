# Book 1 Unit 18 — Around the School

Work began only after Unit 17's publication reported succeeded, 2026-09-13.
Source: supplied ACCC Book 1, Lesson 6 printed pp. 104–112 and 114–115.
Vocabulary pages were rendered to verify Traditional forms and tone-marked
pinyin, including Taiwan dìfāng for 地方. The location-before-action pattern
comes from Dialogue II pp. 107–109. Explanations, sentences and exercises are
original; no textbook imagery or complete dialogues are included.

12 new words/forms: 學校 現在 山 山上 海 風景 美 地方 裡面 外面 圖書館 上課.
13 required first-time characters: 校 現 山 海 風 景 美 地 方 面 圖 館 課.
All are new before this unit, including considering later Book 2 declarations;
no other unit needs reclassification. 山 and 山上 are separate taught forms,
but 山 contributes one character. 面 is not the already taught 麵.

Six lessons plus review, 146 activities. Sequence:
1. School and now, with explicit explanation of 現在在.
2. Mountain, mountain location and the sea; 山上 does not require the summit.
3. Scenery and beautiful; reuse 聽說 from Unit 17.
4. Place, inside/outside, reference point before localizer, and 不在.
5. Library; distinguish known thing + 在 + location from location + 有 + thing;
   introduce 有沒有 / 沒有 existential questions and negatives.
6. Attend class; time before place before action; distinguish 去 from 在.

Review introduces nothing new. All referenced new rules precede assessments,
all ordering phrases have earlier teaching cards, and all writing tasks have
an introduction first. Listening distractors use already introduced characters;
polyphonic 地 is tested with the written context 地方 instead of bare audio.
No relative-clause, verb-reduplication, progressive 在 or 不是-focus-negation
knowledge is assumed. Characters have specific notes, component descriptions,
complete disjoint stroke coverage and all four handwriting stages.
