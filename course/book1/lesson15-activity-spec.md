## 1. Activity-engineering contract

Every important NEW or EXPANSION target must follow:

**EXPLAIN → RECOGNIZE → CONTROLLED PRACTICE → CONTEXTUAL USE → RETRIEVAL → DELAYED RETRIEVAL → CUMULATIVE MIXING**

Every NEW character must additionally receive, in this order before independent writing:
1. recognition/read-aloud support;
2. `intro`;
3. `trace`;
4. `build`;
5. `complete`;
6. `memory`;
7. contextual reading in the owning lesson;
8. later `complete` handwriting retrieval in the unit review;
9. later-unit retrieval when the containing word is cumulatively reused.

No question may require a word, sense, grammar rule, support glyph, or character before its explanation/availability.

Question-correctness rules:
- one defensible keyed answer only;
- Traditional Chinese; no Simplified contamination;
- Taiwan-appropriate usage;
- tone-marked pinyin on phrase/grammar records where the product schema expects pinyin;
- no hidden/deferred glyph in a required answer;
- no answer clue that makes the target trivial for the wrong reason;
- order banks reconstruct the exact intended phrase with exact token multiplicity;
- grammar distractors must differ on the tested restriction, not on unrelated errors.
