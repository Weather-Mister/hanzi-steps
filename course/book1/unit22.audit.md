# Book 1 Unit 22 — A Busy Afternoon

Primary source: supplied *A Course in Contemporary Chinese* Book 1, Lesson 7 printed pp. 135–145.

13 new lexical forms: 午餐, 剛, 下課, 下午, 半, 比賽, 結束, 開始, 寫, 忙, 每, 書法, 課.
12 first-time characters: 剛 半 比 賽 結 束 開 始 寫 忙 每 法.
午, 下, 課, 書 and other prerequisites were already available before this unit; they are not falsely labelled new.

The unit continues directly from Unit 21 and keeps the textbook’s functional progression:
- afternoon and recent-event vocabulary from the second Lesson 7 dialogue;
- 點半 clock time;
- asking and saying when a game starts/ends;
- progressive ongoing 在 before action verbs, contrasted with locative 在;
- 每…都… for habitual activity;
- negative scope with 每…都不/沒… versus 不是每…都…;
- calligraphy-class vocabulary integrated into a daily schedule.

Six teaching lessons plus review. Every new character receives intro, trace, build, completion and memory work before independent review. Grammar is introduced before dependent assessment. Examples and exercises are original.

The remaining Lesson 7 material is intentionally left for the next unit: 最近, 字, 問, 等一下, 有事, 有意思, and the permission use of 可以/不可以/可不可以/可以嗎. This avoids overloading Unit 22 and preserves the mature Hanzi Steps 10–13-word pacing while following the source order.
