## Disposition meanings

Every pedagogically relevant Lesson-15 source item receives exactly one protocol disposition:

- **NEW** — not previously owned/taught in the required Lesson-15 sense; must be taught before productive assessment.
- **KNOWN** — already formally taught with the same learner-usable function and safe for direct reuse.
- **REVIEW** — previously taught and intentionally reused/retrieved in Lesson 15.
- **EXPANSION** — a prior base exists, but Lesson 15 adds a new semantic, structural, pragmatic, or combinatorial extension that requires explicit teaching.
- **DEFERRED** — not promoted to productive Lesson-15 ownership. The reason and destination/condition are explicit.

Source-boundary records **B1L15-SRC001–B1L15-SRC009** are bibliographic metadata rather than learner concepts, so the five pedagogical dispositions do not apply to them.
