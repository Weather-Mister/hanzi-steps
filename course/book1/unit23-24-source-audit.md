# Book 1 Units 23–24 source audit

Authoritative source: supplied *A Course in Contemporary Chinese 1*.

## Unit 23 — Can I Go Check It Out?

Source basis: the remaining Lesson 7 dialogue/vocabulary and the Lesson 7 permission section (printed pp. 135–145). Unit 22 intentionally stopped before 最近, 字, 問, 等一下, 有事, 有意思 and the permission use of 可以; Unit 23 now closes that gap before Lesson 8 begins.

New lexical forms: 最近, 每天, 等一下, 有事, 字, 寫字, 剛開始, 有意思, 問, 不可以, 可不可以, 可以嗎. The exact surface form 可以 is review because it was formally taught earlier for possibility; this unit explicitly teaches its Lesson 7 permission reading.

Checked points:
- 可以 precedes the permitted action.
- 不可以 is taught as the negative permission/prohibition form, matching the textbook's usage note.
- 可不可以 and 可以…嗎 are taught as permission questions in the lesson context; the explanation notes that A-not-A forms can also ask about possibility in other contexts.
- 最近 and 每天 are integrated with already-taught routine grammar rather than presented as new grammar.
- 問問 reviews the earlier softened/reduplicated action pattern instead of falsely introducing a second new rule.
- Every first-time character (字 等 事 意 思) has full guided handwriting practice.

## Unit 24 — How Are You Getting There?

Source basis: Lesson 8's opening transportation material and grammar I–IV (printed pp. 160–174): transportation vocabulary, companionship with 跟, method questions with 怎麼, implicit comparison with 比較, and 又…又….

New lexical forms: 坐, 火車, 跟, 玩, 怎麼, 慢, 比較, 快, 高鐵, 車票, 又…又…, 舒服. Thirteen first-time characters are taught: 坐 火 車 跟 慢 較 快 高 鐵 票 又 舒 服. 玩/怎/麼 are already-known characters and are not relabelled as new.

Checked points:
- 坐 + vehicle is used for taking transportation.
- 跟 + person precedes the shared activity.
- 怎麼 is a question adverb before an action phrase; it is explicitly contrasted with 怎麼樣, which asks for an evaluation.
- 比較 is treated as an implicit comparison; the Taiwan-specific usage note that it retains a comparative meaning is preserved.
- 又…又… coordinates two properties of the same topic.
- Explicit A 比 B + property is no longer stretched into a vocabulary-thin Unit 24. It remains Lesson 8 material for the following unit, where it can be taught with further transportation content and adequate lexical load.
