## 7. Grammar ledger

### B1L15-G001 — Non-committal Stance with Question Words

Source: printed p. 331 / PDF p. 358; English reference printed p. 340 / PDF p. 367.

Function:
- question words in a declarative sentence can express a non-committal stance when the speaker avoids a clear/specific answer.

Source-named forms:
- 什麼
- 多少
- 幾
- 哪裡
- 什麼地方
- 誰
- 什麼時候

Restriction:
- the textbook explicitly states that these non-committal statements are **always negative**.

Core examples:
- 我沒有多少錢。
- 她沒有幾個朋友。
- 你的感冒沒有什麼關係。
- 我沒去哪裡。
- 他不打算買什麼。
- 我昨天沒跟誰去看電影。

Drill:
- answer ordinary information questions with deliberately non-specific negative answers.

Required distinction:
- this is a declarative indefinite/non-specific reading under negation, not a literal information-question use.

### B1L15-G002 — To Dispose of Something with 把

Source: printed pp. 332–333 / PDF pp. 359–360; English reference printed pp. 340–341 / PDF pp. 367–368.

Function:
- introductory disposal construction for what is done with/to an object; the source loosely compares it to “take this noun and …” in English.

Core introductory structure:
- **把 + object + bare outward transitive action verb + 了**

Core examples:
- 我把牛肉麵吃了。
- 他把我的湯喝了。
- 房東把房子賣了。

Source restrictions:
1. The 把 object is in most cases **definite in reference**.
2. Bare nouns or modified nouns such as 這個 / 那個 / 他的 are illustrated.
3. The source rejects `*我想把一支手機賣了` with an indefinite numeral object.
4. The verb in this introductory pattern is a **bare outward transitive action verb**.
5. Source-compatible examples include 賣、吃、喝、寫.
6. The source rejects inward verbs such as 買 and 學; `*我把中文學了` is explicitly unacceptable.
7. The basic illustrated pattern ends in sentence-final 了.

Negation:
- 沒 / 別 precedes 把.
- examples:
  - 我沒把豬腳麵線吃了。
  - 他沒把書賣了。
  - 別把我的藥吃了。

Questions:
- 你把功課寫了沒有？
- 你是不是把機車賣了？
- 你是不是把他的早飯吃了？

Picture drill:
- asks what people did with coffee, a book, xiaolongbao, beef noodles, and a motorcycle.

Required semantic constraint:
- “dispose of / do with / do to” is source-significant; do not reduce G002 to arbitrary object fronting.

### B1L15-G003 — V了…就… “do … right after doing …”

Source: printed pp. 333–334 / PDF pp. 360–361; English reference printed pp. 341–342 / PDF pp. 368–369.

Function:
- event 2 occurs right after completion of event 1.

Core examples:
- 我放了假就去旅行。
- 他吃了藥就睡覺。
- 爸爸回了家就喝了兩杯烏龍茶。
- 他到了臺北，就去臺北101看看。
- 妹妹喝了一碗熱湯，就不覺得冷了。
- 姐姐吃了臭豆腐，肚子就不舒服。

Structure:
- the two events can be affirmative or negative.

Questions:
- A-not-A is explicitly unavailable;
- use 嗎 or 是不是.

Mandatory contrast with Lesson-13 一…就…:
1. 一 is followed by a relatively short verbal phrase and emphasizes immediate succession.
2. V了…就… is less restricted in what can occur in event 1.
3. 以後 may be used with V了…就…:
   - 他吃了藥以後，就去睡覺。
4. The source rejects:
   - `*他一吃了藥以後，就去睡覺。`

Drill prompts include:
- 訂蛋糕
- 裝有線電視
- 放假
- 付學費
- 看紅葉

### B1L15-G004 — 一點 “a bit”

Source: printed pp. 335–336 / PDF pp. 362–363; English reference printed p. 342 / PDF p. 369.

Function:
- indicate minimal quantity or degree.

Structures:

1. **一點 + NP** — minimal quantity
   - 她在超市買了一點東西。
   - 我只喝了一點烏龍茶。
   - 昨天下了一點雨。

2. **Vs + 一點** — post-state-verb modifier indicating comparison
   - 他比我年輕一點。
   - 請你早一點來！
   - 明天我會晚一點回家。

3. **有（一）點 + Vs** — slight degree before a state verb, **not** comparison
   - 牛肉麵有一點辣。
   - 這裡冬天有一點冷。
   - 這支手機有一點貴。
   - 他有（一）點想睡。

Usage:
- 一點 may reduplicate to **一點點**, “just a tiny bit.”

Drill coverage:
- 一點;
- 有一點;
- 多／少 + V + 一點.

Required distinction:
- **Vs + 一點** is comparative;
- **有（一）點 + Vs** is non-comparative slight degree.

### B1L15-G005 — Comparing Actions with a 得 Complement

Source: printed pp. 336–337 / PDF pp. 363–364; English reference printed pp. 342–343 / PDF pp. 369–370.

Function:
- compare actions/outcomes using a 得 complement.

Pattern 1:
- **Comparative + Action + 得 + State**
- 他比我做得快。
- 我比他走得快。
- 弟弟比哥哥念得好。

Pattern 2:
- **Action + 得 + Comparative + State**, with verb repetition where required.
- 他做飯做得比我快。
- 我走路走得比他快。
- 弟弟念書念得比哥哥好。

Usage:
- the textbook states that Pattern 2 is used most frequently.

Negation:
- 不 precedes 比.
- 他做飯不比我做得快。
- 走路，我不比他走得快。
- 弟弟念書念得不比哥哥好。

Questions:
- use 嗎 / 是不是.
- 你做飯做得比媽媽好嗎？
- 他是不是走路走得比你快？
- 弟弟打網球打得比哥哥好嗎？

Drill coverage:
- quantity of coffee/Oolong tea sold;
- snowfall duration;
- KTV singing duration;
- walking speed/time;
- cooking time.

### B1L15-G006 — Complements of Degree in Comparison Structures

Source: printed p. 338 / PDF p. 365; English reference printed p. 343 / PDF p. 370.

Function:
- degree can be preverbally adverbial or postverbally complementary; this lesson focuses on postverbal comparison complements.

Target forms:
- **…一點**
- **…得多**
- **…多了**

Core examples:
- 他的房間比我的大一點。
- 捷運站比公車站遠得多。
- 我覺得晚上比早上舒服多了。

Questions:
- 哥哥是不是比弟弟高一點？
- 房租可以便宜一點嗎？
- 春天去旅行是不是比夏天舒服得多？
- 高鐵票比火車票貴多了嗎？

Drill:
- answer comparison questions using 一點 / 得多 / 多了.

### B1L15-G007 — Separable Verbs

Source: printed pp. 339–340 / PDF pp. 366–367; English reference printed p. 344 / PDF p. 371.

Function:
- separable verbs are presented as a special verb category whose typical internal structure is [V + N];
- V and N may separate and material may be inserted between them.

Source-listed examples:
- 唱歌
- 上班
- 上網
- 上課
- 生病
- 睡覺
- 看書
- 念書
- 滑雪
- 游泳
- 照相
- 吃飯
- 做飯
- 見面

Three separation types:

1. **verbal 了 inserted**
   - 他回了家以後，就開始工作。
   - 我昨天下了課就跟朋友去看電影。

2. **object / recipient inserted**
   - 我想見你一面。
   - 他照了你一張相。

3. **time duration inserted**
   - 我們每天上八個鐘頭的班。
   - 你們新年的時候，放幾天的假？
   - 他唱了三小時的歌，有一點累。

Drill:
- separate V and N for the listed verbs and insert suitable material.

## 8. Pragmatics / source usage

- **B1L15-P001** — **怎麼了** functions as a concern/condition inquiry in the health dialogue.
- **B1L15-P002** — **不用 了** declines offered help/necessity.
- **B1L15-P003** — **好不好** in 我陪你去看病，好不好？ seeks agreement to a proposed helpful action.
- **B1L15-P004** — **最好** and activity-support **應該** are advice/suggestion frames.
- **B1L15-P005** — the lesson outcome and Activity II explicitly require polite **acceptance and rejection of suggestions**.
- **B1L15-P006** — **謝謝妳的關心** is a response to another person’s concern/care.
- **B1L15-P007** — doctor-patient turns use **請問 / 您** as politeness resources.
- **B1L15-P008** — **好一點了 / 好多了 / 好得多了** encode different degrees of improvement and should not be collapsed into one undifferentiated “better.”
- **B1L15-P009** — **油的、冰的東西最好都別吃** combines nominalized state descriptions, totality, advice, and imperative negation.

These records constrain faithful interpretation. They do not create additional formal vocabulary entries.

Source: printed p. 349 / PDF p. 376.
