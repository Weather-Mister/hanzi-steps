## Language and answers

- Taiwan wéibō for 微波; piányí for 便宜; wèishénme; zhǒng for 種 meaning kind.
- 包子 has neutral-tone zi; the standalone 子 card explicitly distinguishes zǐ.
- Natural 一/不 changes are marked in examples; neutral 不 is used in A-not-A.
- 幫 names the beneficiary before the action; negative 不 precedes 幫.
- 二百/兩百 and 二千/兩千 are acknowledged variants, never false distractors.
- Full place-value forms distinguish 105/150, 1,005/1,050, and 1,000/10,000.
- Explicit arithmetic: 80+50=130; 25+25+80=130; 3*25+80=155.
- 的 substitutes for a noun supplied by context; no unsupported singular/plural
  distinction is taught. Plain adjective predicates are distinguished from
  emphatic 是 patterns by the question wording.
- 了 is explained only in 太…了 here, not as a blanket past-tense marker.
- 能 is taught for device capability, without claiming that 能 and 會 never overlap.
- Context-dependent pronunciations use written-reading questions rather than
  relying on a bare-character device voice to disambiguate them.
