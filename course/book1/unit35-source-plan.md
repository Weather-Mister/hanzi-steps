## Grammar II 是…的

The source grammar is treated as a **past-event focus/contrast construction**, not as ordinary copular 是 + 的.

The unit now explicitly represents all source distinctions:
- 是 is placed directly before the focused element;
- 的 closes the construction;
- possible focus: subject, time, place, manner, and occasionally verb;
- object focus is not allowed;
- an object is often moved to the front as topic;
- negation puts 不 before 是;
- yes/no questions can retain 是…的 and add 嗎;
- 是 can sometimes be omitted;
- wh-questions can ask who, when, how, or where about a past event;
- “what” cannot be used when “what” is the object.

Source examples represented directly include:
- 學費是公司替我付的。
- 他是昨天晚上到臺灣的。
- 他是在學校附近吃晚飯的。
- 我是坐公車來上課的。
- 是我打電話給房東的。
- 我不是在圖書館看書的。
- 你的房租是自己付的嗎？
- 我跟朋友一起來的。 (source optional-是 example with 是 omitted)
- 是誰打電話給你的？
- 他是什麼時候來學校的？
- 你是怎麼去的？
- 你是在哪裡吃飯的？

The invalid object-focus restriction is also independently tested rather than being left only in explanatory prose.

## 替 placement

Vocabulary I labels 替 as a preposition “for, on behalf of.” Unit 35 makes its position explicit:
- 替 + person + action
- 公司替我付 = the company pays for/on behalf of me

This is lexical/syntactic support for the source sentence, not a separately invented textbook grammar section.

## Important boundary: 以後

Dialogue I uses 以後 alone as a time expression meaning “in the future.” Unit 35 teaches only that lexical use.

Grammar III later in Lesson 12 teaches Event + 以後 “after…”. That grammar remains deferred to the next unit and is not silently claimed here.

## Handwriting

The nine new characters are 費、司、替、希、望、班、累、加、油. Geometry is hydrated from AnimCJK using exact Unicode codepoints only.

The deep audit also checks structural grouping against Taiwan-standard forms where the first implementation was too coarse:
- 替: 㚘 + 曰; the upper 㚘 is visually two 夫 shapes side by side.
- 班: 王 + two narrow center strokes (丶丿) + 王 in the Taiwan standard form.

These grouping repairs do not alter the underlying stroke geometry or Unicode targets.
