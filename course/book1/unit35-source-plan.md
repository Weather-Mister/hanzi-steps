# Unit 35 source coverage plan

Authoritative source: *A Course in Contemporary Chinese 1*, Lesson 12 「你在臺灣學多久的中文？」, Dialogue I tail and Vocabulary I (printed pp. 246–249), plus Grammar II 是…的 (printed pp. 253–255).

## Scope

Unit 35 completes Dialogue I and teaches the full source 是…的 past-event focus system. It deliberately does **not** advance into Dialogue II beyond what is needed to explain the source grammar.

## Grammar II 是…的

Unit 35 preserves the textbook distinctions:
- function: highlight one element of a past event as focus/contrast;
- base frame: Subject + 是 + Focus + Activity + 的;
- the object is often moved to the front as topic;
- possible focus: subject, time, place, manner, and occasionally verb;
- object cannot be the focus;
- negation is 不 before 是;
- 是 can sometimes be omitted;
- wh-questions can ask who, when, how, or where about a past event;
- the construction cannot ask “what” when “what” is the object.

Source examples represented include:
- 學費是公司替我付的。
- 他是昨天晚上到臺灣的。
- 他是在學校附近吃晚飯的。
- 我是坐公車來上課的。
- 我不是在圖書館看書的。
- 他是什麼時候來學校的？

## Important boundary: 以後

Dialogue I uses 以後 alone as a time expression meaning “in the future.” Unit 35 teaches only that lexical use.

Grammar III later in Lesson 12 also teaches Event + 以後 “after…”. That grammar remains deferred to the next unit and is not falsely claimed here.
