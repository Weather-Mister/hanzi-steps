New characters:
芒、紅、瓜、拍、笑、心、穿、衣

- guessing/confirmation 吧, contrasted with the previously learned suggestion 吧;
- monosyllabic VV看 for tentative trying, including the Lesson 10 object restriction;

水果、黃色、給、香、甜、以前、塊、吧、請.

## First-teaching compatibility migrations

Two characters/words were previously first-declared in existing Book 2 material even though Book 1 Lesson 10 now requires them earlier:

- **紅** moves character first-teaching to Book 1 Unit 30. Book 2 Unit 3 keeps all existing 紅綠燈 lessons, activities and pronunciation instruction, but 紅 becomes review character there.
- **往** moves vocabulary and character first-teaching to Book 1 Unit 31. Book 2 Unit 1 keeps all existing 從…往… route lessons and activities, but 往 becomes review vocabulary/character there.

These are compatibility migrations only. They do not begin or redesign Book 2.
