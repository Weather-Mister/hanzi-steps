# Book 1 Units 28–29 source and coverage plan

## Why this batch was rebalanced

The first published version split Lesson 9 too mechanically and left Unit 28 with five
new lexical items and Unit 29 with six. The user explicitly approved more flexible
unit boundaries. The revised batch therefore keeps every published lesson/step position,
moves the remaining supported Lesson 9 lexical first-teaching into Unit 28, and lets
Unit 29 begin Lesson 10 instead of padding either unit.

This does **not** count transparent combinations as new vocabulary.

## Unit 28 — How Long and When?

New vocabulary/forms (11):
- 影片 yǐngpiàn — film; video
- 年 nián — year
- 日 rì — day/date in written dates
- 天 tiān — day as a standalone duration unit
- 鐘頭 zhōngtóu — hour
- 月 yuè — month
- 號 hào — date/day of month
- 還 hái — still; additionally
- 貓空 Māokōng — Maokong
- 要是 yàoshi — if
- 就 jiù — then; in that case

New characters (8):
- 年、日、鐘、頭、月、號、就、貓

New grammar remains focused on the remaining Lesson 9 time-duration system:
- Time-When vs. Time-Duration.
- Object + repeated verb + duration.
- Duration before negation.
- Duration inside separable verbs.

Unit 28 gives lexical/context preview for 月、號、要是、就 so Unit 29 can teach the
full calendar and condition grammar without carrying a six-word vocabulary load.

Transparent combinations are not counted as new vocabulary: 一年、兩天、三個鐘頭、
九月三十號、放三天的假.

## Unit 29 — If I Have Time, I’ll Try It

Retained Lesson 9 grammar:
- Number + 月 + Number + 號 / 日 calendar dates.
- 要是…就… condition/consequence, including subject placement and negative clauses.

New Lesson 10 vocabulary/forms (11):
- 水果 shuǐguǒ — fruit
- 黃色 huángsè — yellow
- 芒果 mángguǒ — mango
- 給 gěi — give
- 香 xiāng — fragrant
- 甜 tián — sweet
- 西瓜 xīguā — watermelon
- 對 duì — correct; right
- 以前 yǐqián — before; in the past
- 機會 jīhuì — opportunity; chance
- 吃吃看 chīchīkàn — try tasting it and see

New characters (8):
- 水、果、黃、色、芒、給、香、瓜

New Lesson 10 grammar:
- VV看: reduplicated one-syllable action verb + 看 for a tentative “try it and see”
  meaning, including the textbook restriction that VV看 normally does not take an
  object directly after 看.

Sense extension not counted as new vocabulary:
- 塊 is already formally known from prices; Unit 29 explicitly teaches its Lesson 10
  food-piece measure-word use in 給你一塊芒果.

## Explicit carry-forward items

rule: a small, verified source/handwriting blocker may be carried forward explicitly
instead of forcing an underfilled unit.

Lesson 10 is also intentionally incomplete after Unit 29. In particular, **紅色** and
the remaining Dialogue I/Vocabulary I material not yet formalized must be handled by
the next source-aware continuation; do not silently mark Lesson 10 complete.
