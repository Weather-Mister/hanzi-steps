## Unit 28

**How Long Does It Take?**

New vocabulary/forms:
- 影片 yǐngpiàn — film; video
- 年 nián — year
- 日 rì — day/date in written dates
- 天 tiān — day as a standalone duration unit
- 鐘頭 zhōngtóu — hour

New characters:
- 年、日、鐘、頭

New grammar:
- Time-When vs. Time-Duration contrast.
- Object + repeated verb + duration.
- Duration before negation.
- Duration inside separable verbs, with 的 in the practiced forms.

Transparent combinations are not counted as new vocabulary: 一年、兩天、三個鐘頭、放三天的假.

## Unit 29

**If I Have Time**

New vocabulary/forms:
- 月 yuè — month
- 號 hào — date/day of month
- 還 hái — still; additionally
- 貓空 Māokōng — Maokong
- 要是 yàoshi — if
- 就 jiù — then; in that case

New characters:
- 月、號、就、貓

New grammar:
- Number + 月 + Number + 號 / 日 calendar dates.
- 要是…就… condition/consequence, including subject placement and negative conditions/consequences.

## Explicitly deferred material

Lesson 9 is **not declared complete** after Unit 29.

Two source items remain blocked by the handwriting requirement:
- **逛 guàng**. The current AnimCJK Taiwan Traditional dataset used by the repository does not provide an acceptable verified geometry record for first teaching.
- **臺東 Táidōng**. The required traditional character **臺** is not available in the current verified Taiwan Traditional source used by the project.

These items must remain on the coverage map until a licensed Taiwan Traditional source is added and verified. Do not silently substitute simplified/Japanese geometry or change the textbook spelling simply to make validation pass.

Everything else in Lesson 9 Dialogue I/II, Vocabulary I/II, and Grammar I–V is covered by Units 26–29 or by previously taught vocabulary explicitly reused there.
