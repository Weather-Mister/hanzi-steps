# Book 1 Units 28–29 source and coverage plan

## Why this batch was rebalanced

The originally published Units 28–29 were structurally valid but too thin lexically: Unit 28 had 5 new vocabulary/forms and Unit 29 had 6. That happened because a grammar-heavy source boundary was treated too rigidly.

Hanzi Steps unit boundaries are now explicitly **pedagogical rather than mechanical textbook cut points**. A unit may close omitted material from an earlier source section or begin the next textbook lesson when that produces a better learning load, provided source order, prerequisites, first-teaching ownership, handwriting provenance, and saved-progress compatibility remain explicit.

Do not pad a unit with transparent combinations merely to hit a count. Do not create an almost-empty unit merely to stop at a lesson boundary.

## Unit 28 — How Long Does It Take?

### New vocabulary/forms
1. 影片 yǐngpiàn — film; video
2. 年 nián — year
3. 日 rì — day/date in written dates
4. 天 tiān — day as a duration unit
5. 鐘頭 zhōngtóu — hour
6. 非常 fēicháng — very; extremely
7. 但是 dànshì — but; however
8. 站 zhàn — station; stop
9. 或是 huòshì — or
10. 便利商店 biànlì shāngdiàn — convenience store
11. 公共汽車 gōnggòng qìchē — bus
12. 中國 Zhōngguó — China

### New characters
年、日、鐘、頭、非、但、站、或、利、汽

### New grammar
- Time-When vs. Time-Duration contrast.
- Object + repeated verb + duration.
- Duration before negation.
- Duration inside separable verbs.

Transparent combinations such as 一年、兩天、三個鐘頭、放三天的假 are not counted as new vocabulary.

The added Lesson 8 items repair source-coverage omissions rather than inventing extra novelty.

## Unit 29 — If I Have Time

2. 號 hào — date/day of month
3. 還 hái — still; additionally
4. 貓空 Māokōng — Maokong
5. 要是 yàoshi — if
6. 就 jiù — then; in that case
7. 水果 shuǐguǒ — fruit
8. 黃色 huángsè — yellow
9. 給 gěi — to give
10. 香 xiāng — fragrant
11. 甜 tián — sweet
12. 以前 yǐqián — before; in the past

### New characters
月、號、就、貓、水、果、黃、色、給、香

### New grammar
- Number + 月 + Number + 號 / 日 calendar dates.
- 要是…就… condition/consequence, including subject placement and negative conditions/consequences.

The final six lexical items deliberately open Lesson 10 instead of leaving Unit 29 as a six-word unit. No Lesson 10 grammar is pulled forward.
