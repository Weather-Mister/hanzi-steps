# Book 1 Unit 16 — Spicy Food & Cooking Skills

Work began only after Unit 15's deployment reported succeeded on 2026-09-12.
Source: supplied A Course in Contemporary Chinese 1, Lesson 5, printed
pp. 89–95 and 99. Vocabulary and grammar page images were inspected for
Traditional forms, tones and pattern details. Teaching and assessments are
original; no scans or complete textbook dialogues are redistributed.

Six teaching lessons plus review, 135 activities. 13 NEW words/forms:
昨天 餐廳 可是 辣 有一點 怕 所以 自己 做飯 甜點 不錯 得 教.
11 first-time characters: 昨 餐 廳 辣 怕 所 自 己 甜 錯 教.
以 was taught inside 可以; 得 was taught inside 覺得. Their existing cards
and geometry are retained. 得 now receives explicit performance-marker
teaching and two guided handwriting review activities. Every untaught
character in the new vocabulary has a full character card and practice.

Teaching moves from yesterday's restaurant visit and contrasting comments
to spice versus temperature, a mild drawback with 有一點, sensitivity with
怕辣, a reason followed by 所以 and its result, cooking oneself, desserts,
positive 不錯, 得 performance complements, and asking someone to teach.
It distinguishes learned ability with 會 from performance with 得. Objects
after the verb require the taught verb repetition; object-first topics and
understood objects receive separate examples. Negative performance uses
不 after 得, and questions include 怎麼樣 and 好不好. 教 uses jiāo for the
teaching verb. Context-dependent 教 and 得 are tested with written-context
reading questions, not ambiguous bare-character listening prompts.

Dates beyond yesterday, destination 到, and remaining Lesson 5 vocabulary
such as 知道, 一定, 這麼, 小籠包 and 臭豆腐 remain for later units.
No new material is introduced by the review lesson.

Nine new glyphs use unmodified AnimCJK Taiwan records. 廳 and 辣 are absent
from that subset. Their same-form licensed source records (Ja for 廳, Hans
for 辣) are preserved separately. Both were compared visually with Taiwan
MOE stroke-by-stroke. 辣 is unchanged. 廳 retains every source outline and
median, with the ear and lower-left strokes reordered to the Taiwan sequence.
All 11 character cards have specific component descriptions and complete
stroke coverage; the grouped glyph render was visually checked. Attribution
and the original source records are in public/. No MOE artwork is included.

All 15 active targeted checks pass; the pre-existing architecture fingerprint
