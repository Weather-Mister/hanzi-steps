## Reconciled grammar requirements

The following are canonical source requirements.

### B1L14-G001 — Time-Duration after verbal 了

- marks duration of a **completed** activity;
- construction includes verbal 了 followed by a time duration;
- with a transitive verb followed by its object, the verb is repeated;
- when the object/location is fronted or otherwise displaced, the source examples do not repeat the verb;
- questions/drills include 嗎, 是不是, 多久, 幾個月, 幾個鐘頭, and repeated-verb questions.

### B1L14-G002 — Completion-to-date with Double 了

- marks duration accumulated up to the speech point;
- the action may or may not continue depending on context;
- must remain contrastive with G001 rather than being collapsed into one generic duration pattern;
- source drills use 多久了 and contextual 已經.

### B1L14-G003 — 快／要／快要 … 了

- marks imminence / an event or state change about to happen;
- source forms include 快…了, 要…了, 快要…了;
- the textbook states that Taiwan prefers disyllabic 快要;
- explicit time words block 快要 under the source rule, e.g. *他明天快要回來了;
- the drill requires inference from contextual evidence, not generic future reference.

### B1L14-G004 — 比…更…

- 更 means “even more so” relative to an established or understood baseline;
- 更 precedes a state verb;
- this lesson teaches it inside 比-comparisons;
- the source identifies 是不是 as a typical question form;
- the “even” baseline is source-significant and must not be reduced to plain comparative “more.”

### B1L14-G005 — Inferior comparison with 沒有

- canonical form **A 沒有 B 那麼／這麼 + State Verb**;
- normally negative with 沒（有）;
- 那麼 / 這麼 may sometimes be omitted;
- positive **A 有 B 那麼／這麼 + State Verb** is rare except in questions;
- questions use **有沒有**;
- the source explicitly contrasts equal **跟…一樣**, inferior **沒有…那麼**, and superior **比**;
- a question such as 今天有沒有昨天熱？ carries the source-described baseline presupposition that yesterday was fairly hot.
