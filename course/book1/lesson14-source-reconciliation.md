## Reconciled grammar requirements

The following details are source-required and therefore canonical:

1. **B1L14-G001 — Time-Duration after verbal 了**
   - marks duration of a completed activity;
   - primary shape Verb + Time Duration;
   - if a transitive verb is followed by its object, the verb is repeated;
   - fronted/relocated object examples avoid repetition;
   - questions/drills include 嗎, 是不是, 多久, 幾個月, 幾個鐘頭.

2. **B1L14-G002 — Completion-to-date with Double 了**
   - marks duration accumulated up to the speech point;
   - the action may or may not continue depending on context;
   - must be contrasted with G001 rather than taught as the same pattern.

3. **B1L14-G003 — 快／要／快要 … 了**
   - marks imminence / an event or state change about to happen;
   - the textbook states that Taiwan prefers disyllabic 快要;
   - explicit time words block 快要 in the source rule, e.g. *他明天快要回來了.

4. **B1L14-G004 — 比…更…**
   - 更 means “even more so” relative to an established/understood baseline;
   - 更 precedes a state verb;
   - this lesson teaches it inside 比-comparisons;
   - 是不是 is the source’s typical question form here.

5. **B1L14-G005 — Inferior comparison with 沒有**
   - canonical form A 沒有 B 那麼／這麼 + State Verb;
   - normally negative;
   - 那麼 / 這麼 may sometimes be omitted;
   - positive A 有 B 那麼／這麼 + State Verb is rare except in questions;
   - questions use 有沒有;
   - the source explicitly contrasts equal 跟…一樣, inferior 沒有…那麼, and superior 比.
