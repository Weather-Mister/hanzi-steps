## 1. Unit boundaries and overall pacing

The four-unit split is pedagogically justified rather than mechanically following the textbook boundary.

- **Unit 45** isolates symptom and duration language before the denser diagnosis/treatment grammar.
- **Unit 46** completes Dialogue I with diagnosis, medicine, recovery advice, G001, and introductory 把.
- **Unit 47** opens Dialogue II with stomach trouble, help/refusal pragmatics, and one integrated sequencing grammar target.
- **Unit 48** carries the comparison/advice/separable-verb systems and the source transfer tasks after the necessary lexical foundation has accumulated.

A three-unit compression would stack too much new health lexicon and grammar together, while a five-unit expansion would create thin fragments. The proposed four-unit architecture gives each dialogue half a coherent communicative arc.

## 2. Unit 45 — 12 words / 12 characters / no formal Lesson-15 grammar

The flagged risk that Unit 45 might be a character dump does not materialize.

Lesson progression:
1. doctor / discomfort inquiry;
2. runny-nose phrase;
3. head / pain / appetite;
4. throat / inflammation;
5. sick / fever;
6. duration integration and Dialogue-I consolidation.

The lexical load is at the top of the normal target range but is distributed across six lessons and is semantically tight. The absence of a formal grammar target creates room for the 12-character handwriting load and for X002/X004 health-inquiry/duration integration.

The new-character order follows first lexical use:
- 醫;
- 直、流、鼻;
- 痛、胃;
- 喉、嚨、發、炎;
- 病、燒.

Known glyphs such as 頭、水、差 do not create false lexical familiarity; the new word/sense remains explicitly taught.

**Result:** balanced high-character unit, not an artificial vocabulary/character dump.

## 3. Unit 46 — pull-forwards, G001, and G002

### V026 幾 pull-forward

V026 is correctly treated as an EXPANSION rather than prior knowledge. It is introduced in U46-L2 before G001 depends on its non-specific / “a few” value.

The blueprint preserves the distinction between:
- prior interrogative 幾;
- Unit-40 question-word totality with 都 / 也;
- Lesson-15 negative non-committal declaratives.

The dependency edge V026 → G001 is explicit and forward-only.

### V035 別 pull-forward

V035 is correctly pulled forward into U46-L4 because the source G002 system includes imperative negation with 別 + 把. The blueprint places 把 and 別 before G002 within the same teaching lesson and freezes V017 + V035 + X031 + prior 了/negation/question systems as prerequisites.

### G002 prerequisite repair check

A strict Unit-44 learner already has:
- verbal 了;
- sentence-final 了;
- 不 vs. 沒（有）;
- 是不是;
- definite demonstrative/possessive support.

The new prerequisites 把 and 別 are taught in U46-L4 before productive G002 use. The architecture explicitly requires the source sentence-final-了 pattern to be contrasted with the prior rule that negating a completed verbal-了 event removes verbal 了.

The unit also keeps modal 得 děi separate from prior complement 得 de and introduces it with the medicine dialogue before literal productive reuse.

Character order again tracks lesson order:
- 感、冒;
- 藥、局、拿;
- 把;
- 休、息、睡.

**Result:** no hidden prerequisite or grammar-before-teaching defect.

## 4. Unit 47 — repeated events, G003, and help/refusal pragmatics

U47-L2 teaches 次 only after Unit-46 幾 has already been expanded; X008 好幾次 is explicitly available only after both V026 and V027.

G003 appears in U47-L3 after the learner already knows:

**Result:** coherent lexical-to-grammar-to-pragmatic progression.

## 5. Unit 48 — low lexical count vs. four grammar targets

Unit 48 is intentionally below the usual lexical target range:
- 8 NEW formal lexical entries;
- 1 lexical EXPANSION;
- only 1 NEW formal character;
- 4 formal grammar targets.

The unit is conceptually dense, but the six-lesson sequence prevents the four grammar targets from collapsing into one block:

1. advice / concern / recipient 跟 / 回家 / 油 / 冰 / 關心 / 最好;
2. G004 一點 system;
3. G005 action comparison with 得 plus 包 / 睡 / 小時 and X039 分鐘;
4. G006 degree complements plus X035 and A001;
5. G007 separable-verb generalization;
6. prescription support and A003.

The grammar order is pedagogically valid:
- G004 precedes G006;
- G005 precedes A001;
- G005 and G006 remain separate systems;
- G007 is delayed until 生病, 睡覺, 看病, and 回家 are all available.

The sole NEW character 冰 appears in the first lesson, so later lessons do not accumulate handwriting debt.

**Result:** high conceptual load, but not unreasonable overload. The low lexical/character burden is the correct counterweight; splitting the unit would separate tightly related comparison material or produce a thin prescription-only unit.

### A002 — advice / care role-play

U48-L1 teaches 最好, 關心, advice acceptance/rejection, and the new health-specific pragmatics while retrieving prior 應該. A002 is explicitly delayed until those supports are available.

No source proper name or address form becomes productive assessment.

### A003 — prescription

A003 remains last:
- 包 is already taught in U48-L3;
- 次 is known from Unit 47;
- X037 一日4次 / 3日份 is taught;

The learner is therefore reading a supported prescription rather than decoding hidden vocabulary.

### A004 / A005

- A004 follows G002 and its support bundle in Unit 46.
- A005 occurs only after the health-inquiry and symptom systems have accumulated through Unit 47.

**Result:** all source transfer tasks occur after their material prerequisites.

## 7. Dialogue timing

The dialogue split does not force comprehension of future grammar.

- D1T01–D1T05 are integrated only after Unit-45 symptom/duration support is available.
- D1T07 receives lexical preparation before G001, then full use with G001.
- D1T08–D1T09 follow medicine vocabulary and modal 得 support.
- D1T11 follows G002 and the recovery vocabulary.
- D2T02 receives stomach/repetition lexical preparation before full G003 use.
- D2T04–D2T06 wait for refusal and health/insurance vocabulary.
- D2T08–D2T10 wait for the Unit-48 advice/concern targets.
- D2T12 waits for 包 / 睡, G004/P008 support, and G005.
- D2T13 is deliberately split: the degree branch follows G006, while productive separable-verb use waits for G007.

This is source reordering for prerequisite safety, not distortion or unnatural repetition.

The architecture includes meaningful retrieval rather than isolated four-unit silos:

- Unit 46 retrieves Unit-45 生病 / 發燒;
- Unit 47 retrieves the Unit-46 幾 expansion and prior 好不好;
- Unit 48 retrieves 應該, 別, prior comparison grammar, performance 得, 是不是, and prior separable-duration knowledge;

The communicative arc progresses:
symptoms → diagnosis/treatment → stomach trouble/help → advice/recovery/comparison.

No unit boundary interrupts a prerequisite chain.
