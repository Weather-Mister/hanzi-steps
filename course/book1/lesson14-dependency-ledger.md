## Disposition meanings

Every pedagogically relevant Lesson-14 source item receives exactly one of the protocol dispositions:

- **NEW** — not previously owned/taught in the required Lesson-14 sense; must be taught before productive assessment.
- **KNOWN** — already formally taught with the same function and safe for direct reuse.
- **REVIEW** — previously taught and intentionally reused/retrieved here.
- **EXPANSION** — the written form or structural foundation is already known, but Lesson 14 adds a new sense, function, constraint, domain, or combination that requires explicit teaching.
- **DEFERRED** — not promoted to productive Lesson-14 ownership. The reason and destination are stated explicitly.

Source-anchor records B1L14-SRC001–SRC009 are bibliographic metadata rather than learner concepts, so the five pedagogical dispositions do not apply to them.
