# Book 1 Lesson 15 — Curriculum Architecture Pass 2: Refined Split

This pass refines the four-unit architecture selected in lesson15-curriculum-rough.md. It preserves the validated source and dependency ledgers without changing any upstream disposition.

Proposed unit sequence:
- Unit 45 — Symptoms and Feeling Sick
- Unit 46 — Diagnosis, Medicine, and Treatment
- Unit 47 — Stomach Trouble and Getting Help
- Unit 48 — Advice, Comparisons, and Recovery

The sequencing principle is:

1. Unit 45 establishes symptom language and health-duration integration without adding dense formal grammar to the heaviest early character load.
2. Unit 46 finishes Dialogue I, pulls B1L15-V026 and V035 forward only because G001/G002 require them, and teaches the first two formal grammar targets.
3. Unit 47 opens Dialogue II with stomach trouble, help/refusal pragmatics, and G003 after the completed-action and 一…就… prerequisites are already secure.
4. Unit 48 finishes Dialogue II only after the remaining advice, comparison, prescription, and separable-verb prerequisites are explicitly staged.
