## Audit question

Does every required Lesson-14 source item have a justified home in the proposed Units 42–44 architecture, and does the blueprint introduce unsupported material or silently lose source restrictions?

## Findings discovered during the architecture pass and fixed before final verdict

### B1L14-CUR-SF-001 — source transfer instructions needed to be explicit
Severity before fix: **MAJOR**

The first proposed blueprint named B1L14-A001/A004/A005 but did not preserve every source task requirement explicitly enough:
- A001 requires most/least liked season and reasons;
- A004 requires asking for additional information and writing results in the chart;
- A005 requires writing findings in Chinese.

Resolution:
- the final blueprint now states those obligations explicitly;
- A003 also explicitly preserves equal/unequal temperature comparison across last year/this year.

Status: **resolved**.

### B1L14-CUR-SF-002 — culture needed historical-detail preservation
Severity before fix: **MINOR**

The first proposed culture allocation could have been read as only a generic “typhoon day” note.

Resolution:
- Unit 44 now preserves the source reading's historical content at learner-appropriate depth: threshold concept, four-hour window, school/work suspension, and the described central-to-regional decision shift;
- outdated contact details are treated as dated source material rather than current guidance.

Status: **resolved**.
