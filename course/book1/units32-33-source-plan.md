# Units 32–33 source coverage plan

Authoritative source: *A Course in Contemporary Chinese 1*, Lesson 11 「我要租房子」 / “I Would Like to Rent a Place”, Dialogue I/Vocabulary I (printed pp. 224–226), Dialogue II/Vocabulary II (pp. 227–229), Grammar I–V (pp. 230–235).

## Pacing decision

Lesson 11 is split at the dialogue boundary and by communicative load:

- **Unit 32 — A Place to Rent**: all of Dialogue I, Vocabulary I, Grammar I–II.
- **Unit 33 — Settling Into the New Place**: Dialogue II, Vocabulary II, Grammar III–V.

Both units retain the established six-teaching-lessons-plus-review structure and 22-activity reviews. Unit 32 has 14 formal NEW vocabulary targets; Unit 33 has 13. This is deliberate: 再 belongs to the closing line of Dialogue I, while 等 is a genuine source-listed Vocabulary II item and must not be mislabeled as earlier review.

## Grammar coverage

### Unit 32
1. **來 + VP** — intention of coming toward the speaker/relevant location to do something; 不 / auxiliaries / adverbs are placed before 來; contrasted with earlier 去 + VP.
2. **就 — sooner than expected** — time/place-distance context, optional sentence-final 了 where appropriate, and explicit contrast with earlier linking/conditional 就.

### Unit 33
3. **Existential subject with 有** — 有 + indefinite subject + VP; 沒有 negation; questions; and the textbook's focus contrast between location + 有 + NP and 有 + NP + 在 + location.
4. **Different types of 會** — acquired skill vs possibility/likelihood, including negative examples.
5. **Omitting nouns at second mention / zero pronouns** — contextually predictable subject, previously mentioned subject, and previously mentioned object; omission is blocked when the referent would be ambiguous.

## Traditional Chinese / handwriting policy

All learner-facing forms are Traditional Chinese: 廳、廚、間、線、視、關、經、慣、這、裡、話、東, etc. Stroke hydration requests the exact same Unicode character and prefers AnimCJK Traditional data. If a Traditional data row is absent, any geometry fallback must still be for that identical Unicode character; no Simplified character is substituted. Such fallback cases are audited explicitly.
