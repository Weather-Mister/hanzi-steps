# Units 32–33 source coverage plan

Authoritative source: *A Course in Contemporary Chinese 1*, Lesson 11 「我要租房子」 / “I Would Like to Rent a Place”.

## Pacing decision

Lesson 11 is split by communicative load, not by an arbitrary equal-page boundary.

- **Unit 32 — A Place to Rent**: Dialogue I housing/environment language + Grammar I–II.
- **Unit 33 — Settling Into the New Place**: the end of Dialogue I, Dialogue II landlord/problem language + Grammar III–V.

Both units keep the established six-teaching-lessons-plus-review structure. Each has **13 formal NEW vocabulary items**. Transparent combinations, prior vocabulary, semantic extensions, and pragmatic phrases are taught honestly instead of being relabeled as fake novelty.

## Grammar coverage

### Unit 32
1. **來 + VP** — coming toward the speaker/relevant location in order to do an activity; contrasted with earlier 去 + VP. Negatives/auxiliaries/adverbs are explicitly placed before 來.
2. **就 — sooner than expected** — time/distance use, explicitly contrasted with earlier conditional 要是…就….

### Unit 33
3. **Existential subject with 有** — 有 + indefinite subject + VP, 沒有 negation, and the location-topic vs introduced-entity ordering contrast.
4. **Different types of 會** — acquired skill vs future likelihood/will, including negative interpretation.
5. **Omitting nouns at second mention** — recoverable subjects and objects may be omitted; ambiguity is the stopping condition.

## Character / Book 2 first-teaching reconciliation

Lesson 11 first-teaches characters that had previously been declared later in Book 2. Their lesson activities remain intact, but first-teaching ownership moves to the earlier Book 1 occurrence:

- Book 2 Unit 1 → Unit 32: 走、左、右、路
- Book 2 Unit 2 → Unit 32: 超
- Book 2 Unit 3 → Unit 33: 過、再
- Book 2 Unit 3 exact vocabulary 再 also becomes REVIEW there because Unit 33 now formally teaches it first.

No later lesson IDs or learner progress IDs are removed.
