## 3. Exact grammar records

### u48-a-little-degree — G004
- title: **一點 — quantity and degree**
- structures:
  1. **一點 + NP** = a small quantity: 一點東西
  2. **Vs + 一點** = a slightly different degree, often comparative: 好一點 / 早一點
  3. **有（一）點 + Vs** = slight degree, **not** a comparison: 有一點冷
  4. **一點點** = a tiny amount/degree
- required contrast: **Vs + 一點** and **有（一）點 + Vs** are not interchangeable in function.
- source health example: **好一點了嗎？**
- remember: post-state **一點** can express comparative improvement; pre-state **有一點** expresses a slight state.

### u48-action-comparison-de — G005
- title: **Comparing actions with 得**
- Pattern 1: **Comparative + Action + 得 + State**
  - **他比我走得快。**
- Pattern 2: **Action + 得 + Comparative + State**, with verb repetition where required
  - **他走路走得比我快。**
- source says Pattern 2 is most frequent.
- negation: **不 precedes 比**
  - **他走路不比我走得快。**
- questions: use **嗎 / 是不是**.
- source dialogue application:
  - **我吃了一包藥以後，睡得比昨天好。**
- safeguard: this uses complement 得 **de**, not modal 得 **děi**.
- remember: action comparison combines the previously known 得-complement system with 比 comparison.

### u48-comparison-degree — G006
- title: **Degree complements in comparisons**
- target outcomes:
  - **…一點** = slightly more/less
  - **…得多** = much more/less
  - **…多了** = much more/less, with 了
- examples:
  - **他的房間比我的大一點。**
  - **捷運站比公車站遠得多。**
  - **晚上比早上舒服多了。**
- prerequisite: G004 must already be taught.
- safeguard: G006 compares degree of states; do not collapse it into G005 action comparison.
- remember: choose the degree expression according to the intended size of difference and source structure.

### u48-separable-verbs — G007
- title: **Separable verbs and insertion**
- explanation: many source verbs have a V+N internal structure that can separate when material is inserted.
- three source separation types:
  1. **verbal 了 insertion**
     - **他回了家以後，就開始工作。**
  2. **recipient/object insertion**
     - **我想見你一面。**
     - **他照了你一張相。**
  3. **duration insertion**
     - **他唱了三小時的歌。**
     - Lesson-15 application: **睡了幾個小時的覺**
- prior duration insertion from Unit 28 is REVIEW; Lesson 15 expands to explicit category + verbal-了 + recipient/object insertion.
- Lesson-15 forms may be productively split only after lexical availability:
  - 生病 (U45)
  - 睡覺 (U46)
  - 看病 (U47)
  - 回家 (U48-L1)
- safeguard: do not invent productive separation for a word not already lexically available.
- remember: the inserted material goes between the V and N components of a separable form.
