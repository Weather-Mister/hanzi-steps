# Book 1 Unit 20 — Finding Places on Campus

Work began after Unit 19's publication reported succeeded on 2026-09-13.
Primary source: supplied ACCC Book 1, Lesson 6 printed pp. 104–115
(campus vocabulary, locatives, location modifiers and existential sentences)
and pp. 117–118 (corrective 不是). Rendered pp. 106, 108 and 109 confirm
forms and readings, including jiàoshì and the source's yī lóu floor label.
Explanations, teaching sentences and exercises are original, not reproduced
dialogues or textbook exercises.

12 new words/forms: 宿舍 樓 樓下 樓上 棟 大樓 前面 後面 旁邊 教室 游泳池 歡迎.
12 first-time characters: 宿 舍 樓 下 棟 前 後 旁 邊 室 池 迎.
Already taught 歡, 教, 游, 泳 and 上 are reused, not declared new.

Six teaching lessons plus review, 153 activities:
1. Dormitories and numbered floors; 二樓 vs counting objects; first floor
   corresponds to ground level in Taiwan.
2. Relative upstairs/downstairs, location vs movement, and 幾樓 questions.
3. Buildings, measure 棟, 兩棟大樓 vs 二樓, and 哪棟 identification.
4. Front/back relative to a landmark; 圖書館前面 vs 前面的圖書館;
   location + 的 + noun, with the main noun last.
5. Beside vs nearby, classrooms and jiào vs jiāo, correcting an assumed
   location with 不是在 followed by the correct location. Questions do not
   falsely reject ordinary 不在 where both constructions would be valid.
6. Swimming activity vs swimming pool, welcome, and integrated locations.
Review contains no new instruction. Listening choices are all introduced;
all new characters receive introduction, trace, build, completion and memory.
