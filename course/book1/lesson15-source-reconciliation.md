## Reconciled grammar requirements

### B1L15-G001 — Non-committal Stance with Question Words

Canonical source requirements:
- question words in declarative sentences can express a deliberately non-specific/non-committal answer;
- source examples include 什麼、多少、幾、哪裡、什麼地方、誰、什麼時候;
- the textbook explicitly states these non-committal statements are **always negative**;
- the drill converts ordinary information questions into non-specific negative answers.

### B1L15-G002 — To Dispose of Something with 把

Canonical source requirements:
- introductory pattern: **把 + object + bare outward transitive action verb + 了**;
- object is in most cases definite;
- bare nouns / 這個 / 那個 / possessive-modified nouns are illustrated as acceptable;
- indefinite `一支手機` in `*我想把一支手機賣了` is explicitly rejected;
- compatible outward transitive verbs include 賣、吃、喝、寫;
- inward verbs such as 買、學 are explicitly excluded;
- 沒 / 別 precedes 把 in negation;
- questions use 把…了沒有？ and 是不是把…了？;
- “dispose of / do with / do to” semantics are source-significant.

### B1L15-G003 — V了…就…

Canonical source requirements:
- event 2 occurs immediately/right after completion of event 1;
- event clauses may be affirmative or negative;
- A-not-A is explicitly unavailable;
- questions use 嗎 or 是不是;
- contrast with Lesson-13 一…就… must be preserved;
- 以後 may occur with V了…就… but not the source’s 一…就… pattern;
- the drill uses prompts including 訂蛋糕、裝有線電視、放假、付學費、看紅葉.

### B1L15-G004 — 一點

Canonical source requirements:
- minimal quantity/degree;
- **一點 + NP**;
- **Vs + 一點** = comparative “a little more/less…”;
- **有（一）點 + Vs** = slight degree, not comparison;
- 一點點 = “just a tiny bit”;
- exercise contrast also includes 多／少 + V + 一點.

### B1L15-G005 — Comparing Actions with a 得 Complement

Canonical source requirements:
- compares actions/outcomes rather than merely adjective states;
- two source pattern families:
  - Comparative + Action + 得 + State;
  - Action + 得 + Comparative + State, with verb repetition where required;
- Pattern 2 is stated to be used most frequently;
- negation places 不 before 比;
- questions use 嗎 / 是不是;
- source drill covers quantity sold, snowfall/singing duration, walking speed, and cooking time.

### B1L15-G006 — Complements of Degree in Comparison Structures

Canonical source requirements:
- lesson focuses on postverbal degree complements;
- target forms: **一點 / 得多 / 多了**;
- drill requires selecting/using those degree forms in comparisons.

### B1L15-G007 — Separable Verbs

Canonical source requirements:
- separable verbs are presented as a special category, commonly [V + N];
- the source lists 唱歌、上班、上網、上課、生病、睡覺、看書、念書、滑雪、游泳、照相、吃飯、做飯、見面;
- three separation types:
  1. verbal 了 inserted;
  2. object/recipient inserted;
  3. time duration inserted;
- the drill asks learners to separate V and N and insert suitable material.
