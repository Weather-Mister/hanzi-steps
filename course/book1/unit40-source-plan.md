## Grammar IV requirements

Affirmative:
- 誰都…
- 哪裡都…
- 什麼都…
- 什麼時候都…
- 怎麼 + V + 都…

Negative:
- question word + 都 or 也 + 不 / 沒;
- negative marker after 都 / 也;
- total exclusion.

Questions:
- source specifies 嗎.

Exercise demand:
- productive rewrite into all-inclusive/all-exclusive patterns.

## Grammar V requirements

Teach:
- pre-verbal 多 = do more than planned/usual;
- pre-verbal 少 = do less/fewer than planned/usual;
- past or future reference;
- 一點 often modifies the object.

Exercise demand:
- situational suggestions using 多/少 + Verb.
