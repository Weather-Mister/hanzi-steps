# Book 1 Lesson 15 — Curriculum Architecture Pass 1: Rough Split

Inputs:
- validated course/book1/lesson15-source-ledger.md;
- dependency gate PASS in course/book1/lesson15-dependency-ledger.md and course/book1/lesson15-dependency-audit.md;
- cumulative production baseline through Book 1 Unit 44;
- Hanzi Steps pacing: normally around 10–13 genuinely new words/forms per unit, six teaching lessons plus one review, and no prerequisite tested before teaching.

This is Pass 1 only. It chooses candidate Hanzi Steps boundaries and unit numbers but does not freeze Gate A, design activity payloads, or authorize implementation.

## Candidate unit counts considered

### Three units — rejected

Lesson 15 contains:
- 42 formal NEW lexical entries;
- 2 lexical EXPANSION entries;
- 30 NEW formal characters;
- 7 formal grammar targets;
- 5 source classroom-transfer tasks;
- 1 culture section;
- 25 dialogue turns.

A three-unit split would average about 14–15 formal lexical learning targets per unit before counting support expansions, while also forcing multiple dense grammar systems into each unit. That is above the established pacing range and would make the two dialogues, prescription task, comparison work, and character load compete for the same lesson space.

### Five units — rejected

Five units would average fewer than nine formal lexical learning targets per unit and would create at least one thin unit around either the final comparison grammar or the prescription/culture capstone. The source and prerequisite graph support four coherent communicative arcs without manufacturing a grammar-only unit.

### Four units — selected

Four units give a natural dialogue progression and keep the overall lexical load near the established range. The last unit is intentionally below the normal lexical range because it carries four formal grammar expansions, three source transfer activities, prescription support, culture, and the Lesson-15 capstone.

| Unit | Rough arc | Formal NEW | Lexical EXPANSION | NEW formal chars | Formal grammar |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 45 | symptoms, discomfort, illness and duration | 12 | 0 | 12 | none; support integration only |
| 46 | diagnosis, medicine, recovery advice and basic 把 | 10 | 1 | 9 | G001–G002 |
| 47 | stomach trouble, getting help and V了…就… | 12 | 0 | 8 | G003 |
| 48 | advice, improvement, comparisons, separable verbs, prescriptions and capstone | 8 | 1 | 1 | G004–G007 |

Totals:
- formal NEW lexical entries: 42 / 42

- NEW formal characters: 30 / 30

## Deliberate prerequisite pull-forwards

Two source-late lexical items must be taught before their first full grammar ownership:

1. B1L15-V026 幾 “a few / several” remains EXPANSION and is pulled into Unit 46 before G001. G001 explicitly includes the non-specific 幾 branch, so the new sense cannot be left until Dialogue II.
2. B1L15-V035 別 “don’t” remains NEW and is pulled into Unit 46 before G002. Full G002 includes source imperative negation with 別 + 把, so G002 cannot be considered taught before 別 is available.

These are architecture sequencing decisions only. They do not alter the validated dependency classifications.
