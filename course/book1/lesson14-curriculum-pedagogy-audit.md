## Findings discovered during the design pass and repaired

### B1L14-CUR-PED-001 — D1T08 would have exposed G003 before teaching
Severity before fix: **MAJOR**

An earlier refined sequence placed “New Year and Parents” before formal teaching of 快／要／快要…了, even though source turn B1L14-D1T08 contains 新年快到了.

Resolution:
- G003, 快 “soon,” and 要 auxiliary expansion were moved into U43-L3;
- the source turn is presented only after the imminence explanation;
- 停 moved to a later G003-retrieval lesson.

Status: **resolved**.

### B1L14-CUR-PED-002 — D2T07 risked exposing G004 one lesson early
Severity before fix: **MAJOR**

The earlier Unit-44 plan associated D2T07 with the news/caution lesson even though the turn contains 比…更…, formally taught in the following lesson.

Resolution:
- U44-L4 teaches 新聞 / 大家 / 小心 and the safe caution clause 請大家多小心;
- the full B1L14-D2T07 is withheld until U44-L5, after G004 is explained.

Status: **resolved**.

### B1L14-CUR-PED-003 — A002 needed to sit immediately after the duration contrast
Severity before fix: **MINOR**

The first refined split left the experience-transfer activity at the end of Unit 43, separating it from G001/G002.

Resolution:
- B1L14-A002 now occurs in U43-L2 after both completed-duration and duration-to-now have been taught;
- later lessons retrieve the distinction again.

Status: **resolved**.

## Final sequencing audit

### Unit 42

Progression:
1. basic weather;
2. snow/ski;
3. spring/winter;
4. summer/autumn;
5. rain + productive weather questions;
6. season/summer transfer.

Pedagogical result:
- concrete vocabulary precedes open transfer;
- all four seasons are learned as one coherent system;
- 會不會 / 常不常 is explicitly explained before being assessed;
- 因為…所以… is review, not retaught as new;
- deferred names never become prerequisites.

No learner is asked to produce typhoon vocabulary or Lesson-14 duration grammar yet.

### Unit 43

Progression:
1. completed duration;
2. duration to now + 想 “miss”;
3. imminence + New Year/parents;
4. retrieve imminence with 停;
5. end-of-December plans;
6. next-year/autumn-leaf plans.

Pedagogical result:
- G001 precedes G002, making the key semantic contrast teachable;
- B1L14-A002 immediately consolidates that contrast;
- G003 appears before any productive source sentence that depends on it;
- the explicit-time restriction is taught before testing;
- 停 is pre-taught before Dialogue II;
- new-sense 想 / 快 / 要 are treated as expansions, reducing false novelty.

This unit has only 7 formally NEW lexical entries but 3 lexical expansions and 3 grammar systems. Its cognitive load is not thin.

### Unit 44

Progression:
1. umbrella + degree-complement review;
2. typhoon + imminence retrieval + culture frame;
3. wet/annoying + licensed 濕濕的;
4. news/everyone/caution;
5. 更 comparison;
6. 沒有…那麼 comparison + leave-taking.

Pedagogical result:
- Dialogue II can be understood sequentially without future grammar;
- full D2T07 waits for G004;
- D2T08 waits for G005;
- D2T09 reuses already-taught 停;
- caution formulas wait for 大家 / 小心 and prior 多+Verb;
- culture uses English/already-known Chinese where necessary, avoiding hidden language prerequisites.

## Unit-load audit

### Unit 42
- 11 NEW lexical targets
- 8 NEW characters
- no formal new grammar
- one support expansion

Assessment: balanced. Lexically substantive without grammar overload.

### Unit 43
- 7 NEW lexical targets
- 3 lexical expansion targets
- 6 NEW characters
- 3 formal grammar targets

Assessment: grammar-dense but coherent. G001 is an integration of prior systems rather than a wholly novel paradigm, and G001→G002 forms one conceptual pair. The unit remains manageable.

### Unit 44
- 12 NEW lexical targets
- 7 NEW characters
- 2 formal grammar targets
- culture and final capstone

Assessment: upper end of the target but still coherent. Several new exact forms are built from known characters (大家、小心、可怕、這次、上次、慢走), lowering character load. Splitting this into a fourth unit would create thin fragments and break the typhoon episode.

No unit is thin, artificially padded, or overloaded enough to require repartition.

## Review balance

Blueprint requirements enforce:
- direct retrieval of every NEW formal lexical item;
- all assigned grammar targets;
- at least 3 listening activities per review;
- handwriting retrieval;
- at least 18 activities per review;
- cumulative transfer.

Specific delayed retrieval:
- Unit 43 reviews Unit-42 weather/seasons;
- Unit 44 reviews Unit-43 duration/imminence;
- Unit 44 capstone mixes all three units and all four source outcomes.

This is sufficient architecture-level spacing before activity engineering.

## Communicative coherence

- Unit 42 answers “What is the weather/season like, and what do you prefer?”
- Unit 43 answers “How long did/has something happened, and what is about to happen?”
- Unit 44 answers “What is happening with the typhoon, and how do these conditions compare?”

The three arcs are distinct but cumulative.

## Deferral pedagogy

Deferrals are learner-protective rather than source-erasing:
- proper names remain contextual;
- unknown example nouns are substituted only where they are drill scaffolding rather than source targets;
- 呢 is not over-generalized;
- 度 is avoided through °C instead of creating a one-off handwriting burden.

No required grammar or outcome is deferred.
