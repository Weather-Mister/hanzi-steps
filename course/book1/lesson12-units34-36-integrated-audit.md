## Source ledger

### Dialogue I / Vocabulary I

Units 34–35 now preserve the complete dialogue progression:

1. 你計畫在臺灣學多久的中文？
2. 五年。
3. 為什麼要這麼久的時間？

## Grammar I–IV

### I. 先…再…

Unit 34 teaches the textbook's temporal sequence:
- 先 before Event 1;
- 再 before Event 2;
- same-subject omission before 再;
- past and future use;
- the textbook's habitual drill context;
- 再 here as “then / next,” distinct from its “again” use.

The integrated audit restored source past/future examples to active grammar teaching and review.

### II. 是…的

Unit 35 retains all source restrictions and subpatterns:
- subject, time, place, manner, and occasional verb focus;
- object cannot be the focus;
- object/topic fronting;
- 不 before 是;
- yes/no questions with 嗎;
- optional 是;
- who / when / how / where wh-questions;
- rejection of “what” when it is the object.

### III. 以後

Unit 36 correctly distinguishes:
- standalone 以後 = “in the future” from Unit 35 / Dialogue I;
- Event or Time + 以後 = “after / later than …” from Dialogue II / Grammar III.

Both event anchors and time anchors such as 半年以後 are actively retrieved.

### IV. 好 / 難 + verbs

Unit 36 preserves the source's two branches:
- perception compounds: 好吃/難吃、好喝/難喝、好看/難看、好聽/難聽;
- action verbs: 好 = easy to, 難 = difficult to.

It also preserves degree modification, negation, 嗎 questions, branch-specific A-not-A patterns, and the mixed source example 這個歌好聽也好唱, where 好聽 is perception and 好唱 is “easy to sing.”

## Defects found in this integrated audit and repaired

### 1. Two short Dialogue I turns were not actually learner-visible
The source reply 五年。 was only implicit, and 你呢？ at the end of the scholarship turn was omitted.

Repair:
- Unit 34 now explicitly retrieves 五年。 immediately after the opening question;
- the grades/scholarship phrase now ends with 你呢？;
- the review order activity reconstructs the complete turn;
- Unit 35 explicitly marks its opening tuition sentence as the answer to that handoff.

### 2. Grammar I review did not independently retrieve the textbook's past use
The explanation said 先…再… works in past and future contexts, but the review repeated the simplified future study sequence.

Repair:
- review now retrieves 我昨天晚上先寫功課，再看電視。;
- the grammar card restores 我想先吃晚飯，再給媽媽打電話。;
- the existing source future example with the library/supermarket remains.

### 3. One Unit 36 source pinyin string was inconsistent
The source/canonical discourse marker 對了 is represented as duìle; the Unit 36 phrase had Duì le.

Repair:
- corrected the phrase pinyin to Duìle.

### 4. Grammar IV omitted the source sentence that demonstrates both branches together
The rule explained both branches but did not show the source mixed example.

Repair:
- added 這個歌好聽也好唱。 / Zhège gē hǎotīng yě hǎochàng. to the grammar card.
