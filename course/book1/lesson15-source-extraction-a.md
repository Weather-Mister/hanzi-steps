### Dialogue I source functions

The dialogue contextualizes:
- asking where/how someone feels unwell;
- symptom description: runny nose, headache/pain, poor appetite, inflamed throat, fever, cold;
- duration-to-date with 多久了 / 已經…天了;
- non-committal negative with 沒有什麼關係;
- necessity with 得吃藥嗎;
- the basic 把 construction in 把藥吃了;
- suggestions with 多 + V and 早一點 + V;
- doctor-patient politeness with 請問 and 您.

The source uses 妳 for female direct address. Source artifacts preserve that form; any later learner-facing normalization is a downstream decision.

## Grammar I — Non-committal Stance with Question Words

Source: printed p. 331 / PDF p. 358, English reference printed p. 340 / PDF p. 367.

- **B1L15-G001**
- Source label: **Non-committal Stance with Question Words**
- Function: question words in a declarative sentence can express a non-committal attitude when the speaker avoids giving a clear/specific answer.
- The textbook explicitly states that these non-committal statements are **always negative**.

Source question words named:
- 什麼
- 多少
- 幾
- 哪裡
- 什麼地方
- 誰
- 什麼時候

Core examples:
- 我沒有多少錢。
- 她沒有幾個朋友。
- 你的感冒沒有什麼關係。
- 我沒去哪裡。
- 他不打算買什麼。
- 我昨天沒跟誰去看電影。

The drill requires converting ordinary information questions into deliberately non-specific negative answers.

Critical source distinction: these forms are declaratives with indefinite/non-specific readings under negation, not literal information questions.

## Grammar II — To Dispose of Something with 把

Source: printed pp. 332–333 / PDF pp. 359–360, English reference printed pp. 340–341 / PDF pp. 367–368.

- **B1L15-G002**
- Source label: **To Dispose of Something with 把**
- The lesson introduces the basic disposal construction as similar to “take this noun and…” in English, but broader.

Core source structure:
- **把 + object + action verb + 了**

Explicit source restrictions:
1. The 把 object is in most cases **definite in reference**. The source allows bare nouns or modified nouns such as 這個 / 那個 / 他的, but rejects an indefinite numeral phrase such as `*我想把一支手機賣了`.
2. In this introductory pattern, the verb is a **bare, outward transitive action verb**.
3. Source examples of acceptable outward verbs include 賣、吃、喝、寫.
4. The source explicitly rejects inward verbs such as 買 and 學 in this construction, e.g. `*我把中文學了`.
5. The source pattern ends with sentence-final 了.

Negation:
- 沒 or 別 appears **before 把**.
- Source examples include 我沒把豬腳麵線吃了 / 別把我的藥吃了 / 他沒把書賣了.

Questions:
- 把…了沒有？
- 是不是把…了？

Core examples:
- 我把牛肉麵吃了。
- 他把我的湯喝了。
- 房東把房子賣了。

The “dispose of / do with / do to” interpretation is source-significant and should not be reduced to arbitrary object fronting.

## Grammar III — V 了…就… “do … right after doing …”

Source: printed pp. 333–334 / PDF pp. 360–361, English reference printed pp. 341–342 / PDF pp. 368–369.

- **B1L15-G003**
- Function: event 2 occurs right after the completion of event 1.

Core examples:
- 我放了假就去旅行。
- 他吃了藥就睡覺。
- 爸爸回了家就喝了兩杯烏龍茶。
- 他到了臺北，就去臺北101看看。
- 妹妹喝了一碗熱湯，就不覺得冷了。
- 姐姐吃了臭豆腐，肚子就不舒服。

Structural notes:
- the two events can be affirmative or negative;
- the source says the A-not-A form is **not possible** for this pattern;
- questions use 嗎 or 是不是.

Mandatory source contrast with the Lesson-13 **一…就…** pattern:
1. 一 is followed by a relatively short verbal phrase and emphasizes immediate succession.
2. V了…就… is less restricted in what can occur in the first event.
3. 以後 may be used with V了…就…, but not with the source’s 一…就 pattern:
   - 他吃了藥以後，就去睡覺。
   - `*他一吃了藥以後，就去睡覺。`

The drill requires producing the second event from prompts such as 訂蛋糕、裝有線電視、放假、付學費、看紅葉.

## Grammar IV — 一點 “a bit”

Source: printed pp. 335–336 / PDF pp. 362–363, English reference printed p. 342 / PDF p. 369.

- **B1L15-G004**
- Function: 一點 indicates a minimal quantity or degree.

Three source structures:
1. **一點 + NP** — minimal quantity:
   - 她在超市買了一點東西。
   - 我只喝了一點烏龍茶。
   - 昨天下了一點雨。
2. **Vs + 一點** — post-state-verb modifier that indicates comparison:
   - 他比我年輕一點。
   - 請你早一點來！
   - 明天我會晚一點回家。
3. **有（一）點 + Vs** — slight degree before a state verb, **not** comparison:
   - 牛肉麵有一點辣。
   - 這裡冬天有一點冷。
   - 這支手機有一點貴。
   - 他有（一）點想睡。

Usage:
- 一點 can reduplicate as **一點點** for “just a tiny bit”.
- The exercises also contrast 一點 / 有一點 with **多／少 + V + 一點** in suggestions or amount adjustment.

Critical source distinction: **Vs + 一點** is comparative; **有（一）點 + Vs** is non-comparative slight degree.

## Grammar V — Comparing Actions with a 得 Complement

Source: printed pp. 336–337 / PDF pp. 363–364, English reference printed pp. 342–343 / PDF pp. 369–370.

- **B1L15-G005**
- Function: compare actions using a 得 complement.

Two source patterns:
1. **Comparative + Action + 得 + State**
   - 他比我做得快。
   - 我比他走得快。
   - 弟弟比哥哥念得好。
2. **Action + 得 + Comparative + State**, with the action verb repeated where required:
   - 他做飯做得比我快。
   - 我走路走得比他快。
   - 弟弟念書念得比哥哥好。

The textbook states that Pattern 2 is used most frequently.

Negation:
- 不 precedes 比:
  - 他做飯不比我做得快。
  - 走路，我不比他走得快。
  - 弟弟念書念得不比哥哥好。

Questions:
- use 嗎 or 是不是:
  - 你做飯做得比媽媽好嗎？
  - 他是不是走路走得比你快？
  - 弟弟打網球打得比哥哥好嗎？

The exercise compares amount sold, duration of snowfall/singing, speed of walking, and cooking time, so the target must support real action-comparison semantics rather than only adjective comparison.

## Grammar VI — Complements of Degree in Comparison Structures

Source: printed p. 338 / PDF p. 365, English reference printed p. 343 / PDF p. 370.

- **B1L15-G006**
- Function: degree can appear preverbally as an adverbial or postverbally as a complement; this lesson focuses on the postverbal type.

Target complement forms:
- **…一點**
- **…得多**
- **…多了**

Core examples:
- 他的房間比我的大一點。
- 捷運站比公車站遠得多。
- 我覺得晚上比早上舒服多了。

Question examples:
- 哥哥是不是比弟弟高一點？
- 房租可以便宜一點嗎？
- 春天去旅行是不是比夏天舒服得多？
- 高鐵票比火車票貴多了嗎？

The drill requires choosing among 一點、得多、多了 according to the degree being expressed.

## Grammar VII — Separable Verbs

Source: printed pp. 339–340 / PDF pp. 366–367, English reference printed p. 344 / PDF p. 371.

- **B1L15-G007**
- Function: separable verbs are a special verb category whose typical internal structure is [V + N]. The V and N can separate and inserted material can behave as though it occurs between a verb and its object.

The source lists these examples:
- 唱歌
- 上班
- 上網
- 上課
- 生病
- 睡覺
- 看書
- 念書
- 滑雪
- 游泳
- 照相
- 吃飯
- 做飯
- 見面

Three source separation types:
1. **Verbal 了 inserted**
   - 他回了家以後，就開始工作。
   - 我昨天下了課就跟朋友去看電影。
2. **Object/recipient inserted**
   - 我想見你一面。
   - 他照了你一張相。
3. **Time duration inserted**
   - 我們每天上八個鐘頭的班。
   - 你們新年的時候，放幾天的假？
   - 他唱了三小時的歌，有一點累。

The exercise asks learners to separate V and N and insert material for the listed separable verbs. This section systematizes a source property that appears repeatedly elsewhere in the book; later dependency work must distinguish already-known individual words from the new explicit generalization.

## Pragmatic/usage constraints visible in the source

- **B1L15-P001** — 怎麼了 functions as a concern/condition inquiry (“What’s wrong?”), not a literal “how did it become…?” question.
- **B1L15-P002** — 不用了 is used to decline offered help/necessity.
- **B1L15-P003** — 最好 and activity-support 應該 are advice/suggestion frames; the lesson outcome includes accepting and rejecting suggestions politely.
- **B1L15-P004** — 謝謝你的關心 is a response to concern/care, not merely lexical practice for 關心.
- **B1L15-P005** — doctor-patient turns use 請問 and 您 as politeness resources.
- **B1L15-P006** — 好不好 in the offer 我陪你去看病，好不好？ seeks agreement to a proposed action.
- **B1L15-P007** — 好一點 / 好多了 / 好得多了 form a meaningful scale of improvement and must not be collapsed into one undifferentiated “better”.
- **B1L15-P008** — 油的、冰的東西最好都別吃 combines advice, nominalized state descriptions, totality, and imperative negation.
