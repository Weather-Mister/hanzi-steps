# Book 1 Unit 15 — Food Favorites & Ordering

Source: supplied A Course in Contemporary Chinese 1, Lesson 5 pp. 86–88,
plus Lesson 4 pp. 77–78 for 多 with quantities. Vocabulary page images were
checked for Traditional forms and tones. Original teaching, examples and
assessments; no textbook scans or complete dialogues are redistributed.

Six teaching lessons and review: 119 activities. 12 NEW words/forms:
牛肉 麵 好吃 湯 真 小吃 有名 店 最 點 碗 多.
10 first-time characters: 牛 肉 麵 湯 真 名 店 最 點 碗.
好吃 and 小吃 combine earlier characters; 多's earlier character is reviewed.
家 receives an explicitly taught classifier use without counting the same
written word as new again. 牛肉麵 is built from 牛肉 and 麵, not counted twice.

Teaching covers food versus broth taste, 真, description + 的 + noun,
shop classifier 家, 最喜歡, bowl quantities/sizes, food-ordering 點, 很多,
and round-number + 多 versus money-measure + 多. All new words have a
teaching card before their assessments; the final review introduces nothing.
Dates, cooking, 得 complements, spicy-food reactions, 知道, 一定 and other
remaining Lesson 5 material are intentionally reserved for later units.

Accuracy checks include beef versus unspecified bun fillings, Taiwan 20-stroke
麵, exact amount 130+35=165, thirty-something whole-dollar amounts 31–39,
and the distinction between 三十多塊 and 五塊多. Context-dependent meanings
are labeled; 很多 is not confused with 多少. An untaught distractor 名字 was
removed. Listening explanations match the existing bare-character audio flow.

Handwriting geometry has specific component notes and complete stroke coverage.
Nine records use AnimCJK's Taiwan subset. 真 is absent from that subset; its
unchanged same-form Hans record was visually checked, stroke by stroke, against
Taiwan MOE's ten-stroke sequence. Source rows and licenses are retained with
attribution in public/credits.txt; no MOE artwork is redistributed.
