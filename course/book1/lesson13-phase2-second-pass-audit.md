## Five-unit split challenge

The audit attempted to disprove the 37–41 split.

It remains the best allocation.

Why four units is rejected:
- merging the final two slices would put Grammar IV–VII, Dialogue II closure, fixed wishes, cultural comparison, and the full capstone into one unit;
- this would create a much denser grammar load than the established Hanzi Steps unit pattern.

Why six units is rejected:
- the source has only 26 learner-new ordinary Vocabulary I/II forms plus nine Phrase forms;
- another split would create an even thinner lexical unit without resolving a genuine dependency;
- the existing grammar pairings are source-coherent: II+III, IV+V, VI+VII.

Why Lesson 14 is not pulled into Unit 40/41:
- Unit 40 already carries the full totality paradigm + negative system + 多／少 + Verb + birthday culture + 了 contrast;
- Unit 41 carries confirmation pragmatics + the full equality-comparison system + wishes + all four Lesson 13 outcomes;
- Lesson 14 opens a new weather topic, and mixing it before Lesson 13's comparison/wishes capstone would reduce coherence rather than repair pacing.

Conclusion: keep five units.

## Finding 1 — Grammar I needed an explicit negative Event A example

The first blueprint correctly stated that either event in 一…就… may be negative, but its learner-safe example bank only visibly demonstrated a negative Event B.

The source's negative Event A example uses 拍照, whose characters should not be imported casually.

Refinement:
- add learner-safe **那裡一沒人，我就回家。**
- keep **老闆今天早上一到公司，就不開心。** for negative Event B.

Implementation must demonstrate both directions, not merely state the rule.

## Finding 2 — source cultural readings were under-allocated

The first blueprint preserved the lesson's four communicative outcomes but did not explicitly assign every cultural-reading point.

Refinement:

### U40 u40-customs
Include concise learner-safe source culture:
- traditional lunar-calendar birthdays vs. common modern Gregorian-calendar birthdays;
- one-month and one-year infant celebrations;
- special importance of major decades such as 60 / 70 / 80;
- the first-birthday zhuāzhōu “grabbing” custom;
- traditional birthday-food context already supported by 豬腳麵線 / 蛋.

Do not turn culture-reading vocabulary into formal curriculum. In particular, do not expose 抓週 as unscaffolded assessed Hanzi.

### U41 u41-wishes
Include the source pragmatic/cultural point that many congratulatory sayings are fixed four-character auspicious expressions and that 萬事如意 / 心想事成 are presented as broadly useful wishes.

## Finding 3 — integrated activities needed a learner-safe execution contract

## Finding 4 — phrase indexing decision is now explicit

All nine textbook Phrase entries intentionally remain exact first-taught searchable / Mega Challenge forms:

- 生日快樂

- 不必客氣
- 太客氣
- 哪裡哪裡

Rationale:
- they are source-defined pragmatic/lexical targets;
- current Hanzi Steps already indexes useful fixed expressions such as 加油 / 沒問題 / 不好意思;
- current Pinyin Search accepts multiword pinyin and punctuation normalization;
- Mega Challenge eligibility is lesson-completion based;
- repeated characters in a form such as 哪裡哪裡 are deduplicated for per-glyph practice while the full written form remains the vocabulary item.

They remain **Phrase forms in pacing reports**, not nine extra ordinary words.

Excluded from duplicate indexing:
- Name: 西班牙
- review/new-sense forms: 怎麼、那麼、了、對.

## Finding 5 — partial familiarity must not be misrepresented

Two exact forms are first-owned here but contain already-familiar material:

- 語言 is first taught as a standalone vocabulary word in U38, but the learner already encountered 語言中心 in U34.
- 一點 is first taught as the exact quantity form in U39, but the learner already encountered 有一點.

Implementation should acknowledge this continuity rather than present either as completely alien.

This does not remove them from the source inventory or exact-form ownership.
